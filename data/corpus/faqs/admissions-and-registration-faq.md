# Admissions and Registration FAQ

Frequently asked questions about admission to the B.Tech programmes of Sutlej Valley University (SVU) and about registration for courses in each semester, for the academic year 2026-27.

## 1. Eligibility for B.Tech admission

**Q:** What is the minimum qualification to join B.Tech at SVU?
**A:** A candidate must have passed Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry or Computer Science, with at least 60% aggregate marks in these subjects. For candidates from the SC and ST categories, the minimum is 55%.

**Q:** Is there an age limit for admission?
**A:** No. SVU has no upper age limit for B.Tech admission, but a candidate must be at least 17 years old on 1 August of the year of admission.

**Q:** Do I need a gap-year certificate if I passed Class 12 two years ago?
**A:** No separate certificate is needed, but a self-declaration of activities during the gap must be submitted at the time of document verification.

## 2. Programmes, seats and entrance tests

**Q:** Which B.Tech branches does SVU offer?
**A:** SVU offers B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (240 seats), Electronics and Communication Engineering (120 seats), Mechanical Engineering (60 seats) and Civil Engineering (60 seats). This corpus describes the B.Tech programmes only.

**Q:** Which entrance exam scores are accepted?
**A:** SVU accepts a valid JEE Main score. Candidates without a JEE Main score may appear in the SVU Entrance Test (SVUET), held online in May 2026 at designated centres.

**Q:** Is a separate management quota available?
**A:** Up to 15% of seats in each branch are filled through the Management and NRI quota based on Class 12 marks, with the same fee as other students.

## 3. Application process and fee

**Q:** What is the application fee?
**A:** The application fee for 2026-27 is Rs. 1,500, paid online on admissions.svu.example. The fee is not refundable once the form is submitted.

**Q:** What are the application and counselling dates?
**A:** Applications for 2026-27 opened on 15 April 2026 and closed on 30 June 2026. The merit list was published on 6 July 2026, and counselling was held from 10 July to 12 July 2026.

**Q:** How is merit decided?
**A:** Merit is decided on the JEE Main percentile or the SVUET score, along with the Class 12 marks in Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry or Computer Science, in the ratio 70:30.

## 4. Documents required at admission

**Q:** Which documents should I bring for verification?
**A:** Bring the original and one self-attested photocopy of the Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, the JEE Main or SVUET scorecard, the Aadhaar card, the migration or transfer certificate, a medical fitness certificate, the category certificate if applicable, and 6 passport-size photographs.

**Q:** Is an anti-ragging affidavit needed?
**A:** Yes. Both the student and a parent must submit the signed anti-ragging undertaking at admission, as required by the Student Conduct Regulations.

**Q:** Can I submit documents later if I do not have all of them?
**A:** Missing documents can be submitted within 30 days of admission, but admission is cancelled if the Class 12 marksheet is not produced.

## 5. Fees payable at admission

**Q:** How much must I pay when I accept the seat?
**A:** A one-time, non-refundable registration fee of Rs. 10,000 must be paid at counselling to confirm the seat. It is charged separately from the first semester academic fee of Rs. 1,00,000, which is due before course registration.

**Q:** What is the yearly fee?
**A:** The tuition fee is Rs. 1,85,000 per year, and the development and amenities fee is Rs. 15,000 per year, paid in two semester instalments.

## 6. Semester registration and add/drop

**Q:** How do I register for courses each semester?
**A:** Registration is online on the student portal. For the odd semester of 2026-27 it ran from 27 to 31 July 2026, and for the even semester it is from 4 to 8 January 2027.

**Q:** Can I change my courses after registering?
**A:** Yes, during the add/drop window, which stays open for the first two weeks of the semester. For the odd semester it closed on 14 August 2026, and for the even semester it closes on 22 January 2027.

**Q:** Can I register without paying the fee?
**A:** No. Registration is allowed only after the semester fee is paid or an approved instalment plan is in place.

## 7. Lateral entry and change of branch

**Q:** Can diploma holders join in the second year?
**A:** Yes. Diploma holders with at least 60% in a 3-year engineering diploma can take lateral entry directly into Semester 3, and they complete bridge courses in Mathematics and Programming in the first year.

**Q:** Can I change my branch after admission?
**A:** A student may apply for a change of branch after Semester 1 if the CGPA is 8.5 or above and a seat is vacant in the desired branch. Applications must be submitted within 7 days of the Semester 1 result declaration.

**Q:** How many branch changes are allowed?
**A:** Only one change of branch is allowed during the programme.

## 8. Contacting the Admissions Office

**Q:** Where is the Admissions Office?
**A:** The Admissions Office is in Block A, Room A-102. It is open from 9:30 am to 5:00 pm from Monday to Saturday.

**Q:** How can I contact the office?
**A:** Email admissions@svu.example or call 0161-555-0110. Please mention the application number in every email, and expect a reply within 2 working days.

**Q:** Where can I check the status of my application?
**A:** Log in to admissions.svu.example with the application number and date of birth to see the status and download the offer letter.
