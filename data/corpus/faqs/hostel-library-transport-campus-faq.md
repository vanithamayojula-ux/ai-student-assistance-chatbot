# Hostel, Library, Transport and Campus Services FAQ

Frequently asked questions about hostel life, the Central Library, university buses, the health centre and sports facilities at Sutlej Valley University (SVU) for the academic year 2026-27.

## 1. Hostel allotment and rules

**Q:** How do I apply for a hostel room?
**A:** Apply on the student portal under Hostel after confirming admission. Rooms are allotted on merit and distance from home, and first-year students are given priority. The Chief Warden's office is in Block H, Room H-01.

**Q:** What are the hostel in-time rules?
**A:** The hostel gate closes at 10:00 pm every day for all hostels. A late-night pass up to 11:30 pm can be issued by the warden for a valid reason, and a student can get at most 4 late passes in a month.

**Q:** Can parents visit students in the hostel?
**A:** Parents may visit the hostel visitor lounge between 10:00 am and 6:00 pm on any day.

## 2. Hostel and mess fees

**Q:** What are the hostel fees per year?
**A:** For the year 2026-27, the hostel fee is Rs. 62,000 for triple occupancy, Rs. 78,000 for double occupancy and Rs. 98,000 for single occupancy. Mess charges are Rs. 54,000 per year, or Rs. 4,500 per month.

**Q:** Is there a security deposit?
**A:** Yes, a one-time refundable hostel security deposit of Rs. 10,000 is collected at the time of allotment. It is returned within 30 days of vacating the hostel after deducting any damages.

**Q:** Are fees paid per semester?
**A:** Yes. Hostel and mess fees are divided equally in two semester payments and are due on the same date as the academic fee.

## 3. Mess timings and facilities

**Q:** What are the mess timings?
**A:** Breakfast is served from 7:30 am to 9:00 am, lunch from 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm, evening snacks from 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm, and dinner from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm.

**Q:** Is vegetarian and non-vegetarian food available?
**A:** There are two separate mess halls, one purely vegetarian and one with non-vegetarian items on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Sundays. Students can choose their mess at the start of each semester.

**Q:** Whom do I contact for mess complaints?
**A:** Write in the register at the mess office or email the Mess Committee at mess@svu.example. Each mess has a student committee that meets every Saturday.

## 4. Central Library

**Q:** What are the library timings?
**A:** The Central Library in Block A is open from 8:30 am to 10:00 pm from Monday to Saturday, and from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sunday. During end-term examination weeks it stays open until midnight.

**Q:** How many books can I borrow?
**A:** B.Tech students can borrow 4 books at a time for 14 days. A book can be renewed twice if no one else has reserved it.

**Q:** What is the fine for returning books late?
**A:** The overdue fine is Rs. 2 per day per book. A lost book must be replaced or paid for at its current price along with a processing charge of Rs. 500.

## 5. University bus service

**Q:** What are the transport fees?
**A:** The annual bus fee depends on the distance of the pick-up point: Rs. 18,000 for Zone A (within 15 km), Rs. 26,000 for Zone B (15 to 40 km) and Rs. 34,000 for Zone C (beyond 40 km, including Chandigarh and Ludhiana routes).

**Q:** What are the bus timings?
**A:** Morning buses reach the campus by 8:30 am. Evening buses leave the campus at 4:45 pm and 6:15 pm.

**Q:** How do I get a bus pass?
**A:** Apply on the portal under Transport, pay the fee, and collect the pass from the Transport Office in Block B, Room B-03. The pass is valid only with the ID card.

## 6. Campus Health Centre

**Q:** Where is the health centre and when is it open?
**A:** The Campus Health Centre is in Block D and is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm every day. A doctor is available during these hours, and a nurse is on duty at night.

**Q:** What should I do in an emergency?
**A:** Call the 24-hour campus ambulance on 0161-555-0199. Students with serious illness are taken to the tie-up hospital in Ropar.

**Q:** Is there medical insurance?
**A:** Every student is covered by a group accident and hospitalisation insurance policy of Rs. 2,00,000, included in the development and amenities fee.

## 7. Sports and recreation

**Q:** What sports facilities are there?
**A:** The campus has a cricket ground, football field, 2 basketball courts, 4 badminton courts, a table tennis hall and an indoor gym. Use of the facilities is free for all students.

**Q:** When is the gym open?
**A:** The gym is open from 6:00 am to 9:00 am and from 4:00 pm to 9:00 pm every day except on public holidays.

**Q:** How do I join a university team?
**A:** Register with the Sports Officer at the Sports Complex, Room S-01. Trials are held in the second week of August every year.
