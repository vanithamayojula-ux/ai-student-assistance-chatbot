# IT Portal, ID Card, Certificates and Transcripts FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the student portal, campus Wi-Fi, email, identity cards, certificates and transcripts at Sutlej Valley University (SVU) for the academic year 2026-27.

## 1. Student portal login and password

**Q:** How do I log in to the student portal?
**A:** Go to portal.svu.example. The username is your roll number, and the first-time password is your date of birth in the format DDMMYYYY. You must change the password at the first login.

**Q:** I forgot my password. What should I do?
**A:** Use the Forgot Password link, which sends an OTP to your registered mobile number. If the number has changed, email itsupport@svu.example from your SVU email with your roll number.

**Q:** Why is my account locked?
**A:** The account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts. After the lock period ends, you can try again.

## 2. Campus Wi-Fi and student email

**Q:** How do I connect to the campus Wi-Fi?
**A:** Select the network SVU-Campus and log in with your portal username and password. Each student can connect up to 2 devices at the same time.

**Q:** Is there a data limit?
**A:** The fair-use limit is 5 GB per day per student. After this, the speed is reduced to 2 Mbps until midnight.

**Q:** What is my SVU email address?
**A:** Every student gets an email address in the format rollnumber@students.svu.example, with 30 GB of storage. Official notices are sent only to this email, so check it daily.

## 3. IT helpdesk and computer labs

**Q:** Where is the IT helpdesk and when is it open?
**A:** The IT Helpdesk is in Block C, Room C-001, open from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm from Monday to Friday. You can also raise a ticket at itsupport@svu.example.

**Q:** When are the computer labs open for self-study?
**A:** The open computer labs in Block C are available from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm on working days, when no scheduled lab session is running.

**Q:** Is software like MATLAB available?
**A:** Yes. The campus licence gives students free access to MATLAB, Microsoft Office and an engineering design suite. Download links are on the portal under Software.

## 4. Student ID card

**Q:** When will I get my ID card?
**A:** The ID card is issued within 7 working days of completing admission formalities. It is collected from the Student Services Centre, Block A, Room A-105.

**Q:** What is the fee for a duplicate ID card?
**A:** A lost or damaged ID card can be replaced for Rs. 300. Submit a written application with a photograph, and the duplicate is ready in 3 working days.

**Q:** Is the ID card compulsory?
**A:** Yes. It is needed for entry to campus, the library, hostel, bus and examination halls.

## 5. Bonafide, character and other certificates

**Q:** How do I get a bonafide certificate?
**A:** Apply on the portal under Certificates and pay Rs. 100. It is issued in 2 working days and collected from Room A-105.

**Q:** What other certificates are available and what do they cost?
**A:** A character certificate costs Rs. 100, a no-dues certificate is free, and a migration certificate costs Rs. 500 and takes 10 working days.

**Q:** Can the certificate be sent by courier?
**A:** Yes. A courier charge of Rs. 200 within India is added to the certificate fee.

## 6. Official transcripts

**Q:** How can I get an official transcript?
**A:** Apply on the portal under Transcripts. The fee is Rs. 500 per copy, and it is issued in a sealed envelope within 7 working days.

**Q:** Can the transcript be sent directly to another university or employer?
**A:** Yes. Provide the address or email of the receiving institution in the application. A courier charge of Rs. 200 applies for postal delivery within India.

**Q:** Who issues transcripts?
**A:** Transcripts are issued by the Controller of Examinations office, Block A, Room A-114, at coe@svu.example.

## 7. Provisional and degree certificates

**Q:** When will I get my provisional certificate?
**A:** The first copy of the provisional certificate is issued free of cost within 30 days of the declaration of the final semester result, after the no-dues certificates are submitted.

**Q:** When do I get the degree certificate?
**A:** The original degree certificate is given at the annual convocation. Students who cannot attend can collect it later from the Controller of Examinations office.

**Q:** What if the degree certificate is lost?
**A:** A duplicate degree certificate costs Rs. 2,500 and requires an FIR copy and a notarised affidavit.
