# About Lovely Professional University (LPU)

Basic facts about Lovely Professional University (LPU), taken from LPU's official website. Rankings and accreditation grades below are as LPU states them on its own pages.

## 1. Location and history

**Q:** Where is LPU located?
**A:** Lovely Professional University is on the Jalandhar-Delhi Grand Trunk (G.T.) Road, Phagwara, Punjab (India) 144411.

**Q:** When was LPU established?
**A:** LPU's official timeline lists the establishment of Lovely Professional University in 2005. LPU received recognition from the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2006, and began open and distance learning programmes with UGC recognition in 2007.

**Q:** How many students study at LPU?
**A:** LPU's About page describes about 35,000 students from 28 Indian states and over 50 countries, and elsewhere over 30,000 students from 50+ countries, including 3,000+ international students. The figures differ slightly within the page.

## 2. Recognition and accreditation

**Q:** Is LPU recognised by UGC?
**A:** Yes. LPU states that it received UGC recognition in 2006, that it is recognised under Section 12B of the UGC Act, 1956, and that it has UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy.

**Q:** What is LPU's NAAC grade?
**A:** LPU states that it is accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade and a score of 3.68 on a 4-point scale (2023).

**Q:** Is the B.Tech CSE programme at LPU accredited?
**A:** An LPU blog post on B.Tech CSE (October 2025) describes the programme's "NBA-accredited excellence and NAAC A++ rating".

## 3. Rankings

**Q:** What is LPU's NIRF ranking?
**A:** LPU's home page says it ranks 31st among all government and private universities in India in the NIRF Ranking 2025. An LPU blog post (October 2025) says LPU secured 48th in Engineering in NIRF 2025.

**Q:** What other rankings does LPU report?
**A:** LPU's 2026 rankings blog lists: Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026, 5th in India and 501-600 globally; THE Impact Ratings 2026, 1st in India and 23rd globally; WURI 2026, 4th in India and 78th globally. The home page also says 56 LPU faculty members are ranked among the world's top 2% of scientists by Stanford.
