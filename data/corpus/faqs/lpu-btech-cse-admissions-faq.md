# LPU B.Tech CSE Admissions and LPUNEST FAQ

Eligibility, the LPUNEST entrance test and the admission process for B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official programme page and Prospectus 2026. LPU says applicants must read the university website and notices, and that information may change at any time.

## 1. Eligibility

**Q:** What is the eligibility for B.Tech CSE at LPU?
**A:** LPU requires a pass with 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 (with Physics, Mathematics and English) or equivalent, and admission is subject to qualifying through LPUNEST, JEE Main or CUET.

**Q:** Is there any relaxation in the eligibility marks at LPU?
**A:** Yes. LPU gives a 5% relaxation to candidates from the North-East and Sikkim, Defence personnel and their dependents, and wards of Kashmiri migrants. LPU's CSE page also says the English requirement may be waived if the candidate studied in English medium or an equivalent subject.

## 2. LPUNEST

**Q:** What is LPUNEST?
**A:** LPUNEST is LPU's National Entrance and Scholarship Test. It acts as both an entrance test and a scholarship test, and LPU awards scholarships based on LPUNEST scores.

**Q:** How can I take LPUNEST?
**A:** LPUNEST can be taken online, remotely proctored from home, or at a test centre.

**Q:** What is the LPUNEST application fee?
**A:** The LPUNEST application fee is Rs. 1000. It is non-refundable in all cases.

**Q:** Can I get admission to LPU B.Tech with JEE Main or CUET instead of LPUNEST?
**A:** Yes. LPU's B.Tech CSE page says admission is subject to qualifying through LPUNEST, JEE Main or CUET, and the prospectus says national-level tests such as CUET and JEE (Main) are also considered for eligibility for certain programmes.

## 3. Admission process

**Q:** What is the admission process for B.Tech at LPU?
**A:** B.Tech is a Type A programme at LPU. Applicants must qualify LPUNEST and take part in online counselling, where they fill in their preferences and seats are allotted. After seat allotment, applicants apply for provisional admission online at https://admission.lpu.in, or in person at the admission office in Phagwara or a designated camp office.

**Q:** How many counselling rounds does LPU hold for B.Tech?
**A:** LPU's B.Tech CSE page shows five counselling schedules: Schedule I in January, Schedule II, then Phase III in August, Phase IV in September and Phase V in October.
