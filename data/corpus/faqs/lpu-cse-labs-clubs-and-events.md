# LPU CSE Labs, Clubs and Technical Events FAQ

Computer labs, coding spaces, industry tie-ups and technical events for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) students at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's School of CSE page and official LPU news.

## 1. CSE labs at LPU

**Q:** What special computer labs does LPU have for CSE?
**A:** LPU's School of CSE page lists: the iMac Lab, for iOS app development using macOS, Xcode and Swift; the IoT Bot Lab, for software bots, IoT, data analysis and real-time system integration; the Google Lab, offering certifications in mobile app development and digital marketing with access to Google Cloud and G Suite; the Linux Lab, for open-source software, Linux-based development and system administration; and the E-Cell Logic Room, a space for team projects and developing ideas with peers.

**Q:** What is the Capgemini Code Experience centre (CODEX) at LPU?
**A:** It is a coding facility at LPU designed as a real workplace model, with collaboration hubs, discussion zones, coding bays and digital displays. It supports over 200 students at a time and is used for live projects, coding challenges, hackathons and team assignments.

**Q:** Which companies does LPU's School of CSE work with?
**A:** LPU's CSE page shows industry tie-ups with Google, Microsoft, Cisco, Intel, Informatica, TCS, GitHub, Honeywell, IBM, AWS, Quantiphi, upGrad and Kalvium, among others, and memberships such as IEEE, NASSCOM and CII.

## 2. Clubs at LPU

**Q:** Which coding clubs are there at LPU?
**A:** LPU's official CSE pages mention student societies and clubs in general but do not list specific CSE club names. The page titled CODEX Club describes the Capgemini Code Experience coding facility rather than a student club.

## 3. Technical events and competitions at LPU

**Q:** What technical events does LPU's School of CSE hold?
**A:** LPU's CSE page lists events including ICCS-IRICT 2025 (the 10th International Conference on Computing Sciences), a CodeChef workshop, TechFluence 5.0, DevFest Jalandhar 2025, an e-waste awareness and collection programme, and a visit by Charles Severance.

**Q:** What is TechnOcean at LPU?
**A:** TechnOcean was a 3-day tech fest of LPU's School of CSE held on 14 to 16 April 2023 with the theme "Learn. Build. Present." It included a Web3 hackathon, a capture-the-flag (CTF) competition, a Git and GitHub session, an IoT device development workshop, a startup expo, pitch decks, quizzes and a gaming arena.

**Q:** Which coding competitions do LPU students take part in?
**A:** An LPU blog post on B.Tech CSE (October 2025) mentions Smart India Hackathon, CodeVita and Google Summer of Code, over 100 workshops and competitions a year, and practice on HackerRank, Codeforces and LeetCode.
