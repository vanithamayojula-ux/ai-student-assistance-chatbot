# LPU CSE Placements and Career Services FAQ

How placements work at Lovely Professional University (LPU) and what LPU reports about placement results for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE). Placement figures are LPU's own claims. They differ between LPU's pages and between batches, so each figure below is given with the page it comes from.

## 1. Placement process at LPU

**Q:** Who handles placements at LPU?
**A:** LPU's Division of Career Services connects students with recruiters and supports them in securing job offers. Its Centre for Professional Enhancement (CPE) runs placement training.

**Q:** When does placement training start at LPU?
**A:** LPU's placements page says placement training begins from the very first year of the programme. Students first take a diagnostic test of their English and aptitude, then continue to receive training and assessments through a non-credited Professional Enhancement Program (PEP).

**Q:** What does the Centre for Professional Enhancement (CPE) at LPU offer?
**A:** CPE offers soft-skills training, training by industry experts, value-added courses including GATE, UGC NET, UPSC and SSC preparation, career counselling, company-specific mock tests (on platforms such as EXAMLY, HITBULLSEYE, MYPERFECTICE and OAS) and regular tests on AMCAT, CoCubes, eLitmus and Mercer Mettl.

**Q:** Who is eligible for placements at LPU?
**A:** LPU's placements page says students must meet the minimum eligibility criteria set by each company and opt in for placement support. LPU's prospectus says the university may also set conditions such as a specific CGPA and/or a maximum number of backlogs or re-appears to take part in placement activities. A single CGPA cut-off for all placements is not published.

## 2. Placement results reported by LPU

**Q:** What is the highest package at LPU?
**A:** It depends on the page and the batch. LPU's Placement Day release (January 2026) reports the highest package of the Classes of 2025 and 2026 as Rs 2.5 crore, received by a B.Tech (Robotics) student of the Class of 2025. LPU's placements page reports a highest domestic package of Rs 62.72 lakh and a CTC of more than Rs 3 crore with a company in Germany. LPU's CSE school page shows a highest salary package of Rs 64 lakh.

**Q:** What were LPU's placement results for the Classes of 2025 and 2026?
**A:** LPU's Placement Day release (12 January 2026) reports: 2,236 students with packages of Rs 10 lakh or more; an average of Rs 12.20 LPA for the top 10% of the Class of 2026; 6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies; 1,000+ companies offering above Rs 5 LPA; 948 students with more than one offer; and three crore-plus offers for the Class of 2025.

**Q:** Which companies made the most offers to LPU students?
**A:** In LPU's January 2026 release, Accenture made 805 offers, Capgemini India 578 and Cognizant 263. The release also reports Microsoft packages up to Rs 53 LPA (including three intern pre-placement offers at Rs 53 LPA) and a Google package of Rs 58 LPA for the Class of 2025.

**Q:** What are the CSE placement figures on LPU's CSE school page?
**A:** LPU's School of CSE page (no year stated) shows a highest salary package of Rs 64 lakh, an average package of Rs 14.97 lakh for the top 10%, 2,600+ dream and super dream offers, and 1,500+ recruiters who hired LPU students.

**Q:** What university-wide figures does LPU's placements page show?
**A:** For batches 2021 to 2026, 2,000+ LPU students secured packages of Rs 10 lakh to Rs 2.5 crore. The page also states 2,225+ recruiters, 6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies, 400+ recruiters of IITs, IIMs and NITs who also hire from LPU, and an average salary of Rs 10.23 lakh for the top 25% of placed students.

## 3. Recruiters

**Q:** Which companies recruit CSE students from LPU?
**A:** Recruiters shown on LPU's CSE school page include Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Bosch, Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, PhonePe, Intuit, ServiceNow, Cognizant, TCS, Capgemini, Goldman Sachs, Informatica, Accenture, Samsung, Hitachi, Blenheim Chalcot, MoveInSync and Optmyzr. LPU's January 2026 release also names Trilogy Innovations, Juspay, Silicon Labs, PlaySimple Games, Autodesk and Amazon.
