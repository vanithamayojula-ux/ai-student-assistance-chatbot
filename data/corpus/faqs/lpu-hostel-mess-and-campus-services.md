# LPU Hostel, Mess and Campus Services FAQ

Hostels, mess, library, medical care, transport and other services at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official Prospectus 2026 (charges are for session 2026-27 and subject to change) and LPU's residence page.

## 1. LPU hostels

**Q:** Does LPU have hostels on campus?
**A:** Yes. LPU has separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus. The residential facility is given for one year at a time on a first-come-first-served basis, and is not a right.

**Q:** What facilities do LPU hostel rooms have?
**A:** Rooms have an attached washroom (except dormitories), a bed with mattress, table, chair and almirah, and are air cooled or AC as opted. Geysers give hot water in winter at scheduled times. Power comes through dedicated 24-hour lines; backup covers light load only. Wi-Fi is available at scheduled times for academic purposes only. Free parking is available at designated places for residents.

**Q:** What are the LPU hostel charges for 2026-27?
**A:** Per session, air-cooled / AC: 6, 7 or 8 seater Rs 80,000 / Rs 90,000; 5 seater Rs 80,000 / Rs 90,000; 4 seater Rs 93,000 / Rs 1,03,000; 3 seater Rs 1,08,000 / Rs 1,23,000; 2 seater Rs 1,23,000 / Rs 1,43,000; 1 seater AC Rs 1,63,000. With the Early Decision Benefit (opting on or before 30 April 2026) each rate is Rs 10,000 lower. Apartments (4, 3, 2 or 1 seater) cost Rs 1,10,000, Rs 1,30,000, Rs 1,50,000 or Rs 2,00,000.

**Q:** How long can I stay in the LPU hostel each year?
**A:** The hostel facility runs until the end of the regular session including exams, or 10 months from the start of the session, whichever is earlier. Students may arrive two days before the session starts and must vacate within two days after it ends.

## 2. LPU hostel rules

**Q:** What are the main LPU hostel rules?
**A:** Entry of outsiders is restricted, and parents, guardians and visitors need the warden's permission. The university may inspect rooms, bags and almirahs at any time. Posters on walls, incense and burning materials are not allowed, and students pay for any damage. Staying away from the hostel without permission can lead to disciplinary action, including cancellation of the hostel facility.

**Q:** Which electrical appliances are allowed in LPU hostels?
**A:** A kettle, iron, steamer, laptop, mobile charger, shaving set, trimmer, hair straightener and hair dryer are allowed. Heaters, convectors and immersion rods are not allowed, and induction plates are prohibited except in apartments.

**Q:** Can I lose my LPU hostel room?
**A:** Yes. A student who does not report within 3 days of the session start may lose the allotted room. Students with attendance or CGPA below the prescribed limit, or with pending disciplinary cases, may be debarred from the hostel.

**Q:** What are the LPU hostel in and out timings?
**A:** LPU's prospectus and residence page do not publish hostel in-out timings or a curfew; ask the hostel warden.

## 3. LPU mess

**Q:** What food is served in the LPU mess?
**A:** Only vegetarian meals. Students can choose the university mess plan or manage on their own. Menu options are the Standard Mess or Ala Carte, with North Indian, South Indian, Rajasthani, Haryanvi and North-Eastern cuisines (regional cuisine at specific locations only). The standard menu covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and tea with snacks, in unlimited quantity for most items.

**Q:** What are the LPU mess charges for 2026-27?
**A:** Per academic session: Standard mess with two meals Rs 40,000, three meals Rs 44,000, four meals Rs 48,000, and Ala Carte Rs 56,000.

## 4. Library, medical care and transport at LPU

**Q:** What are the LPU library timings?
**A:** LPU's prospectus says the library is open till late hours, presently from 8.00 am till midnight. LPU's residence page separately advertises 24x7 library access.

**Q:** Is there a hospital on the LPU campus?
**A:** Yes. LPU has a 30-bedded hospital within the campus with 24-hour medical and ambulance services. Its staff include resident medical officers, specialists, nurses, dieticians, physiotherapists and psychologists.

**Q:** Does LPU have bus transport?
**A:** Yes. LPU's transport facility is given for one year at a time on a first-come-first-served basis. University buses normally arrive at the campus at 8:45 am and leave at 5:30 pm, unless announced otherwise. Routes and pick-up points may change, and the 2026-27 transport charges are to be updated on LPU's admission page.

## 5. Other campus services at LPU

**Q:** What other facilities are on the LPU campus?
**A:** LPU's campus has branches of 5 nationalised banks, 55+ ATMs, a post office, the Uni Mall and more than 30 restaurants. LPU's residence page mentions over 400 full-time security officials and thousands of security cameras.

**Q:** How much do the LPU gym and laundry cost?
**A:** Gym membership is Rs 6000 for day scholars and Rs 5000 for residents (Rs 4000 with the Early Decision Benefit). Laundry is optional at Rs 5,400 per session. Sports memberships are paid separately, for example badminton or volleyball Rs 3000 and swimming Rs 7000 for day scholars or Rs 6000 for residents.
