# LPU Libraries and E-Resources FAQ

Libraries, collections, timings, online catalog (OPAC), remote e-resources and inter-library loan services at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official Libraries page.

## 1. Library branches and collection

**Q:** Where is LPU's Central Library and how large is the collection?
**A:** LPU has 12 library branches with the Central Library located in Block-37, covering over 10,000 sq.mt. of total library area. The system holds more than 5 lakh books and e-books (including 3 lakh+ e-books), over 60,000 print and electronic journals, 2,000+ newspapers, 800+ magazines, 3,000+ rare books and manuscripts (including Sanskrit, Apbhransh and Pali manuscripts), theses and dissertations, audio-visual material, and study carrels.

**Q:** What library management system does LPU use?
**A:** LPU uses the KOHA Integrated Library Management System (ILMS) with RFID and barcode systems for inventory and security.

## 2. Timings and services

**Q:** What are the book circulation, OPAC and documentation service timings at LPU?
**A:** According to LPU's Libraries page:
- **Circulation of books (issue and return):** 8:00 am to 9:00 pm.
- **Online Catalogue (OPAC / Library Search):** 24x7x365 via UMS (`Library Search`).
- **Documentation Service (photocopy, scanning, typesetting, designing and printing at discounted rates):** 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
- *(Note: LPU's Prospectus 2026 lists overall Central Library reading hours from 8:00 am till midnight.)*

**Q:** Can I borrow books that LPU's library does not own?
**A:** Yes. LPU provides an Inter-Library Loan (ILL) service through DELNET (Developing Library Network, New Delhi). Requests mentioning the author, title, publisher, year and edition can be emailed to `central.library@lpu.co.in`. For general library enquiries, call `01824-444 45634`.

## 3. E-resources, remote access and digital repository

**Q:** Which online journals and databases does LPU provide, and how do I access them from off campus?
**A:** Subscribed databases and e-resources include IEEE, Springer, ProQuest, EBSCO, JSTOR, J-Gate, Manupatra, SCC Online, Capitaline, EPWRF India Time Series, DELNET, Shodhganga, Shodhgangotri, INFLIBNET and the National Digital Library (NDL). On campus, students access them via UMS (`UMS Navigation -> Library Search -> My Links` or `E-Resources`). Off campus, remote access is provided via Mapmyaccess single sign-on using existing library credentials.

**Q:** Does LPU host an open digital repository or Self Learning Materials?
**A:** Yes. LPU manages an institutional DSpace digital repository (`http://dspace.lpu.in:8080/jspui`) and provides open access to Self Learning Materials (SLMs) at `https://www.lpu.in/libraries/slm.php`.
