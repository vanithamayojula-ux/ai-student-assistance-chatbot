# LPU Sports, NCC and NSS FAQ

Sports achievements, National Cadet Corps (NCC) units and National Service Scheme (NSS) community initiatives at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official Sports and Community Services pages.

## 1. Sports at LPU

**Q:** What major university sports achievements has LPU reported?
**A:** LPU's Sports page reports 3,100+ participations and 400+ medals in national and international sports events, finishing 1st Runner-up in the MAKA Trophy 2024 and 1st Runner-up in the Khelo India University Games for three consecutive years.

**Q:** Which notable athletes and sports scholarships are featured on LPU's sports page?
**A:** LPU's Sports page highlights Olympic gold medallist Neeraj Chopra (Tokyo 2020), Indian Men's Hockey Team Captain Harmanpreet Singh (Paris 2024 Olympic bronze and Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Awardee), Mirabai Chanu, Jaismine Lamboria, Balraj Panwar, Dhiraj Bommadevara, Elavenil Valarivan, Sajan Prakash, and the "LPU Dhoni Scholarship" launched by MS Dhoni.

## 2. National Cadet Corps (NCC)

**Q:** How is NCC organised at LPU?
**A:** LPU NCC operates under the motto "Unity and Discipline" and is affiliated with four Jalandhar-based units: 8 PB Battalion, 2 PB Girls Battalion, 2 PB Boys Battalion and 1 PB Air Squadron. It has 542 NCC cadets supported by 2 Associate NCC Officers (ANOs) and 2 Cadet Training Officers (CTOs). Cadets attend camps including CATC, PDLS, Pre-RDC, Pre-TSC, the Republic Day Parade, All India Thal Sainik Camp and the Youth Exchange Program.

## 3. National Service Scheme (NSS)

**Q:** What is LPU-NSS and what activities does it run?
**A:** The National Service Scheme (NSS) under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports has operated at LPU since 2011 with the motto "NOT ME BUT YOU" and 1,900+ active members. LPU-NSS runs SAMARPAN blood donation drives (recipient of the National Blood Donation Award and State Award for Blood Donation), Red Ribbon Club AIDS awareness rallies, Summer and Winter Seven Days Special Camps in adopted villages, the Gyandeep Teaching Project, Feeding Bellies, SATHI Project, Computer Literacy Programme, Joy of Giving, NARI menstrual hygiene awareness, Pulse Polio campaigns and plantation drives.
