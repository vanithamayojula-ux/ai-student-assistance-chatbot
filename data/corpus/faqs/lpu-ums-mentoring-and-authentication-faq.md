# LPU UMS, Mentoring and Document Authentication FAQ

LPU's University Management System (UMS) and Learning Management System (LMS), student mentoring and psychological counselling services, and online degree/transcript authentication, from LPU's official pages.

## 1. UMS and LMS

**Q:** What is UMS at LPU and what services does it include?
**A:** UMS (University Management System, accessible at `https://ums.lpu.in/lpuums`) is LPU's in-house ERP platform offering over 1,000 functionalities for students, parents and staff. Key modules include the Learning Management System (LMS, covering assignments/academic tasks, attendance management and timetables), Relationship Management System (RMS), Examination System, Attendance, Courses, E-Notice, Fee Details, Library, Lab Manuals, Placements, Policies & Rules, Residential Service, Syllabi and Transportation.

**Q:** What campus IT infrastructure supports UMS and Wi-Fi at LPU?
**A:** LPU's UMS page reports 55+ high-end servers, 70 km of fibre optic cable, 25 Gbps internet bandwidth and 3,000+ Wi-Fi access points.

## 2. Mentoring, remedial support and counselling

**Q:** How does LPU's student mentoring programme work?
**A:** Every LPU student is paired with a dedicated mentor for one-on-one academic and personal guidance, progress tracking and regular Parent-Teacher Interactions. Students needing extra help with difficult courses receive Additional Tutorial Classes, Remedial Sessions and Self-Learning Materials.

**Q:** Is mental health counselling available at LPU?
**A:** Yes. LPU's Mentoring and Advising page states that counselling services by psychologists are available round the clock with free and confidential sessions.

**Q:** How do I contact the Division of Student Relationship (DSR) at LPU?
**A:** The Division of Student Relationship is located at Block 32, Room No. 100, and can be reached at `01824-520150` or `dsr@lpu.co.in`.

## 3. Degree and transcript authentication

**Q:** How can an employer or university verify an LPU degree or academic transcript?
**A:** LPU provides an online authentication portal at `https://webapp.lpu.in/authenticate/`. Users select the document type (`Degree`, `Academic Transcript`, or `Lovely Centre for Skill Development`) and enter the student's Registration Number and Document/Certificate Number.
