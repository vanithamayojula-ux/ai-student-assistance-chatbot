# Academic Calendar 2026-27

Notice No. SVU/ACAD/2026/014, dated 15 June 2026, issued by the Office of the Registrar, Sutlej Valley University (SVU). This notice gives the academic calendar for the odd semester (August to December 2026) and the even semester (January to May 2027) for B.Tech programmes.

## 1. Odd semester 2026-27 key dates

The last date for payment of the odd semester fee is 20 July 2026. Online course registration for the odd semester is open from 27 July 2026 to 31 July 2026 on the student portal.

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Odd semester fee last date | 20 July 2026 |
| Online course registration | 27 to 31 July 2026 |
| Commencement of classes | 3 August 2026 (Monday) |
| Add/drop of courses closes | 14 August 2026 |
| Mid-term examinations | 21 to 26 September 2026 |
| Last day of classes | 27 November 2026 |

First-year students attend an orientation programme from 27 July 2026 to 1 August 2026 before the classes begin.

## 2. Mid-term examinations of the odd semester

The mid-term examinations of the odd semester will be held from 21 September 2026 to 26 September 2026. Each mid-term paper carries 20 marks and lasts 90 minutes, as per the Examination and Grading Regulations.

The detailed mid-term timetable will be published on the student portal by 7 September 2026. Normal classes are suspended during the mid-term week. A student who misses a mid-term because of illness must submit a medical certificate within 3 days to be considered for a make-up examination, which is held within 10 days of the original date. Internal marks for the mid-term are displayed on the portal within 7 days of each paper.

## 3. Holidays and breaks in the odd semester

The university will remain closed on the following dates in the odd semester:

| Occasion | Date |
|---|---|
| Independence Day | 15 August 2026 (Saturday) |
| Gandhi Jayanti | 2 October 2026 (Friday) |
| Dussehra break | 19 to 21 October 2026 |
| Diwali break | 7 to 11 November 2026 |
| Guru Nanak Gurpurab | 24 November 2026 |

The hostels remain open during the Dussehra and Diwali breaks, but the mess runs on a reduced menu and students staying back must inform the warden. Classes lost to holidays are not rescheduled unless announced by the Dean Academics.

## 4. End of the odd semester and winter break

The last day of classes in the odd semester is 27 November 2026. A preparatory leave of 9 days is given from 28 November 2026 to 6 December 2026. The end-term examinations will be held from 7 December 2026 to 22 December 2026.

The winter break runs from 23 December 2026 to 8 January 2027. The results of the odd semester end-term examinations will be declared on 8 January 2027. The window for requesting re-evaluation is 7 days from the result declaration, so it closes on 15 January 2027. Students must not leave the campus before their last end-term paper.

## 5. Even semester 2026-27 key dates

Online course registration for the even semester takes place from 4 January 2027 to 8 January 2027. The last date for the even semester fee payment is 31 December 2026.

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Even semester fee last date | 31 December 2026 |
| Online course registration | 4 to 8 January 2027 |
| Commencement of classes | 11 January 2027 (Monday) |
| Add/drop of courses closes | 22 January 2027 |
| Mid-term examinations | 22 to 27 February 2027 |
| Last day of classes | 30 April 2027 |
| End-term examinations | 10 to 25 May 2027 |

## 6. Holidays and events in the even semester

The following holidays and events are listed for the even semester of 2027.

| Occasion | Date |
|---|---|
| Republic Day | 26 January 2027 |
| CodeSprint 2027 hackathon | 20 and 21 February 2027 |
| Utsav annual cultural and technical fest | 4 to 6 March 2027 |
| Holi | 22 March 2027 |
| Baisakhi | 14 April 2027 |

Regular classes will not be held on the three days of the Utsav fest, and attendance for those days will not be counted in the total number of classes. Students who volunteer for Utsav may claim duty leave subject to the Attendance Regulations.

## 7. Summer break and special backlog examination

The summer break begins on 26 May 2027 and ends on 31 July 2027. The Special Backlog Examination for graduating students who have at most 2 backlogs will be held from 12 July 2027 to 17 July 2027, as provided in the Examination and Grading Regulations.

Students must complete their summer internship of at least 6 weeks during the break. The next academic session 2027-28 begins on 2 August 2027. Hostels close on 5 June 2027 for maintenance and reopen on 25 July 2027, and students who need accommodation in July for the special examination should apply to the Chief Warden.
