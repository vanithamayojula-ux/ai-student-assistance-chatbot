# End-Term Examination December 2026 Notice

Notice No. SVU/COE/2026/031, dated 26 October 2026, issued by the Controller of Examinations, Sutlej Valley University (SVU). This notice covers examination form registration, fees, hall tickets and the timetable for the odd-semester end-term examinations of December 2026.

## 1. Examination dates

The end-term examinations for the odd semesters (Semesters 1, 3, 5 and 7) of all B.Tech programmes will be held from 7 December 2026 to 22 December 2026. Examinations will be held on all days from Monday to Saturday as per the timetable, and no examination is scheduled on Sunday.

The classes of the odd semester end on 27 November 2026 and the preparatory leave is from 28 November 2026 to 6 December 2026. The results will be declared on 8 January 2027. Students are advised to check the full timetable for their semester on the student portal.

## 2. Examination form registration and fees

Every student who intends to appear in the December 2026 end-term examinations must fill the online examination form on the student portal between 2 November 2026 and 14 November 2026.

The examination fee is Rs. 1,800 per semester for regular students. Students who are appearing in reappear (backlog) courses must pay Rs. 1,000 per course in addition. The fee can be paid online by UPI, net banking or card, and the fee receipt must be downloaded and kept until the hall ticket is received. A form without the fee payment is not treated as submitted.

## 3. Late registration and late fee

Students who miss the regular window of 2 to 14 November 2026 may still register by paying a late fee. From 15 November 2026 to 19 November 2026, the late fee is Rs. 1,000 in addition to the examination fee. From 20 November 2026 to 21 November 2026, the late fee is Rs. 3,000.

No examination form will be accepted after 21 November 2026 under any circumstance, and a student who has not registered will not be allowed to appear in the December examinations. Such a student will have to take the courses as a reappear in the next cycle with the reappear fee of Rs. 1,000 per course.

## 4. Eligibility to appear

A student is eligible to appear in the examination of a course only if the following conditions are met:

- The student has at least 75% attendance in that course, or the shortage has been condoned by the Dean Academics.
- The student has paid the odd semester fee, which was due on 20 July 2026, and has no pending dues.
- The student has registered on the portal for the course and the examination.

Applications for condonation of attendance shortage must reach the Dean Academics office by 30 November 2026. Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible, as per the Attendance Regulations.

## 5. Hall tickets

Hall tickets will be released on the student portal on 1 December 2026 at 5:00 pm. Students must download and print the hall ticket, and carry it along with the SVU identity card to every examination.

If there is a mistake in the name, photograph or course list on the hall ticket, the student must inform the Examination Cell, Block A, Room A-114, by email at coe@svu.example before 4 December 2026. Hall tickets will not be sent by post. A duplicate hall ticket, if lost, is issued at the examination cell for Rs. 200 on production of the identity card.

## 7. Instructions for the examination day

Students must report to the examination hall by 9:30 am, which is 30 minutes before the start. Entry is closed at 10:30 am, and a student who arrives after that time is not allowed to sit the paper. No student may leave the hall during the first 60 minutes of the examination.

Mobile phones and all electronic devices are banned from the hall, and only a non-programmable scientific calculator is permitted when the question paper allows it. Any use of unfair means is dealt with under the Student Conduct and Unfair Means Regulations. Seating plans are displayed on the notice board outside each examination block 30 minutes before the paper.

## 8. Contact for examination queries

All queries about the examination form, fee payment, hall tickets and the timetable should be sent to the Controller of Examinations at coe@svu.example. The Examination Cell is in Block A, Room A-114, and the counter is open from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm on all working days from Monday to Friday.

During the examination period, the cell will also remain open on Saturdays from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. The telephone number of the cell is 0161-555-0120. Students should mention their roll number, programme and semester in every email, and should expect a reply within 2 working days.
