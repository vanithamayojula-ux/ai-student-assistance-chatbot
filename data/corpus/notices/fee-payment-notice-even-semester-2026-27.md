# Fee Payment Notice for Even Semester 2026-27

Notice No. SVU/ACC/2026/047, dated 1 December 2026, issued by the Accounts Office, Sutlej Valley University (SVU). This notice gives the fee structure, deadlines, late fee and payment methods for the even semester (January to May 2027) of B.Tech programmes.

## 1. Fee payable for the even semester

The fee payable by every B.Tech student for the even semester of 2026-27 is given below.

| Item | Amount per semester |
|---|---|
| Tuition fee | Rs. 92,500 |
| Development and amenities fee | Rs. 7,500 |
| Total academic fee | Rs. 1,00,000 |

The annual tuition fee is therefore Rs. 1,85,000 and the annual development and amenities fee is Rs. 15,000. Hostel, mess and transport fees are charged separately and are listed in the later sections of this notice. Scholarship holders will see their waiver adjusted in the fee statement on the portal.

## 2. Last date of payment

The last date for payment of the even semester fee without any late fee is 31 December 2026. Payment received on or before this date is accepted without penalty.

The fee must be paid before online course registration, which runs from 4 January 2027 to 8 January 2027, because registration is allowed only after the fee has been paid or an approved instalment plan is in place. Classes of the even semester begin on 11 January 2027. Students who have not paid by the last date are not blocked from the portal immediately, but the late fee starts from 1 January 2027.

## 3. Late fee

If the fee is paid after 31 December 2026, a late fee of Rs. 200 per day is charged for each day of delay, up to a maximum of Rs. 2,000. The maximum late fee is reached after 10 days of delay, which is on 10 January 2027.

From 11 January 2027, the student's registration is put on hold and the student cannot attend classes without written permission from the Registrar. The late fee must be paid together with the semester fee, and no late fee is charged on the amount of any scholarship or waiver already sanctioned. Requests for waiver of the late fee are considered only for documented medical or family emergencies.

## 4. Instalment facility

Students who cannot pay the full semester fee at once may opt for the instalment facility on the student portal before 31 December 2026. The fee is paid in two instalments: 60% by 31 December 2026 and 40% by 31 January 2027.

A processing fee of Rs. 1,000 is charged for the instalment facility. If the second instalment is delayed beyond 31 January 2027, the late fee of Rs. 200 per day, up to Rs. 2,000, applies on the unpaid balance. A student who misses both the instalment dates will not receive the hall ticket for the even semester end-term examinations of May 2027.

## 5. Modes of payment

The fee can be paid online on the student portal by UPI, net banking, debit card or credit card. There is no extra charge for UPI and net banking, and a charge of 1.5% is added for credit cards.

Offline payment is also accepted by demand draft drawn in favour of "Sutlej Valley University" payable at Ropar, submitted at the Accounts Office, Block B, Room B-12, between 10:00 am and 4:00 pm from Monday to Friday. Cash and personal cheques are not accepted. The roll number must be written on the back of the demand draft.

## 6. Hostel, mess and transport fees

Hostel residents pay the following per semester, in addition to the academic fee.

| Item | Amount per semester |
|---|---|
| Hostel, triple occupancy | Rs. 31,000 |
| Hostel, double occupancy | Rs. 39,000 |
| Hostel, single occupancy | Rs. 49,000 |
| Mess charges | Rs. 27,000 |

Students using the university bus pay the transport fee for the semester at half of the annual rate, as given in the FAQ on campus services. The hostel and mess fee must be paid by the same date of 31 December 2026.

## 7. Scholarship adjustment

Students who have been awarded a scholarship or the Merit-cum-Means Grant should check the fee statement on the portal before paying. The Accounts Office adjusts the scholarship amount automatically if the sanction letter has been received from the Scholarship Cell.

Results of the Merit-cum-Means Grant applications are expected on 15 December 2026, so grant holders will see a credit of Rs. 20,000 against the even semester fee after that date. Students who have been sanctioned the grant after paying the fee will get a refund to their bank account within 21 working days.

## 8. Receipts and queries

A fee receipt is generated automatically on the student portal immediately after a successful online payment, and it can be downloaded under the Fee tab. Receipts for demand drafts are issued within 3 working days.

For queries about fee statements, payment failures and instalments, contact the Accounts Office at accounts@svu.example or call 0161-555-0130. If an online payment is debited from the bank account but not shown on the portal, send the transaction reference number to the same email address, and the Accounts Office will resolve it within 5 working days.
