# LPU 12th Annual Convocation Notice 2026

Official announcement and statistics for Lovely Professional University's (LPU) 12th Annual Convocation, along with student registration, hall ticket and helpdesk details.

## 1. Event summary and degrees awarded

**Q:** When was LPU's 12th Annual Convocation held and how many degrees were awarded?
**A:** Lovely Professional University held its 12th Annual Convocation on 9 January 2026 at its campus. The ceremony honoured Gold Medallists, PhD scholars and meritorious students of the Class of 2024 and 2025 from Regular, Distance, Online and Part-Time programmes, and was graced by Shri C. P. Radhakrishnan (Hon'ble Vice President of India), Rt Hon. Mary Elizabeth Truss (Former Prime Minister of the UK) and Shri Gulab Chand Kataria (Hon'ble Governor of Punjab).

| Degree category | Degrees awarded |
|---|---|
| Regular programmes | 21,667 |
| Distance programmes | 24,384 |
| Online programmes | 3,762 |
| PhD | 863 |
| Part-Time programmes | 10 |
| **Total** | **50,686** |

## 2. Registration, hall ticket and contact

**Q:** How do students check their convocation schedule or download their hall ticket?
**A:** Students use the LPU Convocation portal (`https://www.lpu.in/convocation/`) with their registration number to check their convocation date and shift, register online, download or reprint their Convocation Hall Ticket, and download their payment receipt.

**Q:** Who do I contact for convocation queries at LPU?
**A:** Contact the Convocation helpdesk at `+91-1824-406659` or `convocation@lpu.co.in`.
