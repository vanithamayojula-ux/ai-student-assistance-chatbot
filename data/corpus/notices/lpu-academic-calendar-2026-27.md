# LPU Academic Calendar 2026-27 (Regular Programmes)

Key dates of the Autumn term (Term-I) 2026-27 at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official academic calendar for regular programmes. LPU states that examination dates are tentative and a variation may occur.

## Autumn term (Term-I) 2026-27 dates at LPU

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Classes start | Monday, 27 July 2026 |
| Mid Term Test (MTT) | 1 to 9 October 2026 |
| Term break | 7 to 10 November 2026 |
| Close of term | Friday, 11 December 2026 |
| End Term Examination (ETE) | 14 to 30 December 2026 |
| Winter vacation | 31 December 2026 to 10 January 2027 |
| Spring term (Term-II) starts | Monday, 11 January 2027 |

**Q:** When are the LPU end-term exams in December 2026?
**A:** LPU's End Term Examination (ETE) for the Autumn term 2026-27 is scheduled from 14 to 30 December 2026. The dates are tentative and may vary.

**Q:** When does LPU's winter vacation start?
**A:** LPU's winter vacation runs from 31 December 2026 to 10 January 2027, and the Spring term (Term-II) starts on Monday, 11 January 2027.

**Q:** When were the LPU mid-term tests?
**A:** The Mid Term Test (MTT) for the Autumn term 2026-27 at LPU was scheduled from 1 to 9 October 2026.
