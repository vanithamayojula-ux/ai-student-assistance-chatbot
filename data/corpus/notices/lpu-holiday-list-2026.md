## Special schedule timings and notes

**Q:** Are there half-days or altered university timings on Raksha Bandhan or Karva Chauth at LPU?
**A:** Yes. On Raksha Bandhan (Friday, 28 August 2026), LPU starts at 10:00 am. On Karva Chauth (Thursday, 29 October 2026), LPU closes at 3:00 pm.

**Q:** What happens if LPU closes on an unscheduled day?
**A:** LPU's holiday notice states that if the university remains closed due to any unforeseen reason, the following Sunday or any Sunday/second Saturday may be declared a working day to compensate for academic loss. The date for Id-ul-Zuha is also subject to moon visibility.
