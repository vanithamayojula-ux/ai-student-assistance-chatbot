# LPU Placement Results Notice, Classes of 2025 and 2026

Lovely Professional University (LPU) announced its placement results for the Classes of 2025 and 2026 on Placement Day, in an official release dated 12 January 2026. The figures below are as reported by LPU.

## Highlights reported by LPU

- Highest package: Rs 2.5 crore, received by a B.Tech (Robotics) student of the Class of 2025.
- 2,236 students received packages of Rs 10 lakh or more.
- The top 10% of the Class of 2026 averaged Rs 12.20 LPA.
- 6,000+ offers from Fortune 500 companies, and 1,000+ companies offered packages above Rs 5 LPA.
- 948 students received more than one offer.
- The Class of 2025 had three crore-plus offers, including Rs 1 crore and Rs 1.03 crore.

## Big tech offers reported by LPU

- Microsoft: packages up to Rs 53 LPA. Three interns were converted to pre-placement offers at Rs 53 LPA with a monthly stipend of Rs 1.25 lakh, and three Class of 2025 students received Rs 51 LPA.
- Google: Rs 58 LPA (Class of 2025).

## Offers by recruiter

| Recruiter | Offers |
|---|---|
| Accenture | 805 |
| Capgemini India Pvt. Ltd. | 578 |
| Cognizant | 263 |

Other recruiters named in the release: Trilogy Innovations, Juspay, Silicon Labs, PlaySimple Games, Autodesk, Amazon and Palo Alto Networks.
