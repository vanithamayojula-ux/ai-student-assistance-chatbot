# Scholarship Applications and CodeSprint 2027 Hackathon Notice

Notice No. SVU/SA/2026/022, dated 9 November 2026, issued by the Office of the Dean Student Affairs, Sutlej Valley University (SVU). This notice announces the application window for the Merit-cum-Means Grant and the registration for the CodeSprint 2027 hackathon.

## 1. Merit-cum-Means Grant application window

Applications are invited from continuing B.Tech students for the Merit-cum-Means Grant for the even semester of 2026-27. The online application form is open on the student portal from 16 November 2026 to 30 November 2026.

Applications received after 30 November 2026 will not be considered. The grant amounts to Rs. 40,000 per academic year, of which Rs. 20,000 will be credited against the even semester fee. The Scholarship Cell, Block A, Room A-122, will hold help desks on 17 and 24 November 2026 from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm to guide students in filling the form.

## 2. Eligibility for the Merit-cum-Means Grant

To be eligible, a student must satisfy all of the following:

- The annual family income is below Rs. 4,00,000.
- The CGPA at the end of the previous semester is at least 7.0.
- The attendance is at least 75% in all courses of the current semester.
- The student has no disciplinary action pending against him or her.

First-semester students are not eligible for this grant, as it is meant for continuing students with a CGPA record. Students who are already receiving an Entrance Merit Waiver of 30% or more cannot receive this grant at the same time.

## 3. Documents required for the grant

The following documents must be uploaded in PDF format, with each file below 1 MB:

1. Income certificate issued by a competent authority (tehsildar or SDM) after 1 April 2026.
2. Marksheet of the previous semester.
3. Aadhaar card of the student.
4. First page of the bank passbook or a cancelled cheque of the student's account.
5. A self-declaration that the information is correct.

Incomplete applications are rejected without notice. A student who submits a false income certificate loses the grant and faces disciplinary action under the Student Conduct Regulations.

## 4. Results and payment of the grant

The list of selected students will be published on the student portal on 15 December 2026. The Scholarship Committee, chaired by the Dean Student Affairs, takes the final decision, and its decision is final.

Selected students will see a credit of Rs. 20,000 in the fee statement for the even semester, which has a payment deadline of 31 December 2026. The remaining Rs. 20,000 will be credited to the odd semester fee of 2027-28. The grant continues for the next year only if the student continues to meet the eligibility conditions and applies again.

## 5. CodeSprint 2027 hackathon dates and venue

CodeSprint 2027 is the annual 24-hour hackathon of SVU, to be held on 20 February 2027 and 21 February 2027. The event starts at 10:00 am on Saturday and ends with the demonstrations and prize distribution at 11:00 am on Sunday.

The venue is the Innovation Hub, Block C, Room C-201, with overflow seating in the Central Auditorium. The problem statements will be announced on the first morning of the event. Food, snacks and a rest area will be provided for all participants for the whole duration. Participants must bring their own laptops.

## 6. Team formation and registration for CodeSprint

CodeSprint is open to all SVU students from any year and branch. Each team must have 3 or 4 members, and a student can be a part of only one team. Teams with members from different branches and years are encouraged.

Registration is free of charge for SVU students and must be completed by 31 January 2027 at codesprint.svu.example. A team that registers late can be accepted only if seats remain. The first 80 teams to register will be confirmed, and the rest will be placed on a waiting list. Each team must nominate a team leader whose email address is the contact point.

## 7. Prizes and duty leave for CodeSprint

The total prize pool of CodeSprint 2027 is Rs. 2,00,000. The first prize is Rs. 1,00,000, the second prize is Rs. 60,000 and the third prize is Rs. 40,000. All finalist teams get a certificate and the winning team gets a chance to present the project to local industry partners.

Participation in CodeSprint can be counted as duty leave under the Attendance Regulations, up to a maximum of 10% of the total classes of a course, if the student obtains prior approval from the Dean Student Affairs. Approval requests should be made by 12 February 2027.

## 8. Contact for the notice

Questions about the Merit-cum-Means Grant should be sent to scholarships@svu.example or taken to the Scholarship Cell, Block A, Room A-122, which is open from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm on all working days. Questions about the hackathon should be sent to codesprint@svu.example.

The student coordinators of CodeSprint 2027 are Aarav Mehta and Ishita Verma of the Coding Club. The Coding Club office is in the Student Activity Centre, Room S-05.
