# Leave, Medical Leave and Grievance Redressal Policy

This policy explains how B.Tech students of Sutlej Valley University (SVU) may apply for leave, how medical absence is handled, and how they can raise complaints and have them resolved in the academic year 2026-27.

## 1. Casual leave for students

A student may take up to 5 days of casual leave in a semester for personal reasons such as a family function. The student must apply on the student portal to the class coordinator at least 1 day in advance, except in an emergency.

Casual leave is a permission to be absent, but it does not add to the attendance of the student. Absent days are still counted as absence under the Attendance Regulations, and the 75% minimum attendance rule continues to apply. Leave approved in advance can, however, support an application for condonation of attendance shortage between 65% and 75%.

## 2. Medical leave procedure

A student who is ill must inform the class coordinator by email on the first day of absence. On rejoining, the student must submit a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner to the Dean Academics office within 7 days.

For illness requiring hospitalisation, the discharge summary must be attached, and the Dean Academics office must be informed within 3 days of the admission. Medical leave is also recorded as absence for attendance purposes, but it is the basis for condonation of shortage between 65% and 75%. A student who is ill for more than 15 consecutive days should consider a break of study under the Academic Progression Regulations.

## 3. Leave for hostel residents

Hostel residents who want to leave the campus overnight must get a leave card signed by the warden at least 24 hours before leaving. For a stay of more than 3 days outside the campus, the written consent of a parent or guardian, sent by email from their registered address, is also required.

Students must return by the gate closing time of 10:00 pm on the day mentioned in the leave card. A student who stays out without a leave card is liable to a fine of Rs. 500 for the first time. The warden informs the parents in the case of repeated violations.

## 4. Compassionate leave

Compassionate leave of up to 7 days is allowed for the death of an immediate family member (parent, sibling, spouse or grandparent). The student must inform the class coordinator or the Dean Student Affairs within 3 days of the event and submit a supporting document within 7 days of rejoining.

This leave does not reduce the attendance requirement. In case of a long period of absence, the Dean Student Affairs may recommend to the Dean Academics that the condonation rules be applied. Students who face such a situation during the examination period should contact the Controller of Examinations at coe@svu.example immediately.

## 5. Grievance redressal in three stages

Students may raise complaints about teaching, assessment, marks, hostel, transport, fees or any other university service. The process has three stages. At Stage 1, the complaint is made to the course instructor or class coordinator, who must respond within 5 working days.

At Stage 2, if the student is not satisfied, the complaint goes to the Head of Department, who replies within 7 working days. At Stage 3, the matter goes to the Grievance Redressal Committee, which gives a decision within 15 working days. Complaints about non-academic services can be submitted directly at Stage 3. Every complaint is given a reference number on the portal.

## 6. Grievance Redressal Committee

The Grievance Redressal Committee (GRC) is chaired by the Dean Student Affairs and has 3 faculty members, 1 member of the administrative staff and 2 student representatives. The office of the GRC is in Block A, Room A-120.

Complaints are sent to grievance@svu.example or dropped in the grievance boxes placed outside the library and the main hostel. The committee meets every second Wednesday and also holds special meetings for urgent cases. The identity of the complainant is kept confidential, and anonymous complaints with supporting evidence are also examined.

## 7. Complaints of harassment

Complaints of sexual harassment, discrimination or bullying are handled by the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), which is headed by a senior woman faculty member and includes an external member from a women's organisation. Complaints can be sent to icc@svu.example or made in person at Block A, Room A-121.

The ICC completes the enquiry within 60 days of receiving the complaint, and the proceedings are fully confidential. Interim relief, such as a change of class section or hostel room, can be given on request. Retaliation against a person who has complained is a serious offence and is punishable under the Student Conduct Regulations.

## 8. Appeal to the Student Ombudsperson

A student who is not satisfied with the decision of the Grievance Redressal Committee can appeal to the Student Ombudsperson within 30 days of receiving the decision. The appeal is sent in writing to ombudsperson@svu.example.

The Ombudsperson, who is an external senior academic appointed by the Vice-Chancellor, gives a final decision within 30 days of receiving the appeal. The Ombudsperson cannot reconsider marks awarded in examinations, as these are dealt with through the re-evaluation process of the Examination and Grading Regulations. The decision of the Ombudsperson is binding on the university.
