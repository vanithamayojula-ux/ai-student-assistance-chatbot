# LPU B.Tech Fees, Scholarships and Refund Policy

Programme fee, scholarships and the refund policy for B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Lovely Professional University (LPU), for 2026 admission, from LPU's official pages and Prospectus 2026. LPU's CSE page says it currently shows the Admission 2026 fee and that the Admission 2027 fee is to be updated soon. Fee tables on LPU's website change by session, so confirm current amounts there.

## 1. Programme fee

**Q:** What is the fee for B.Tech CSE at LPU?
**A:** LPU's B.Tech CSE page gives the programme fee as Rs 160000 per semester for 2026 admission, before any scholarship. Additional charges such as the examination fee and uniform fee may also apply.

**Q:** What does the LPU programme fee include?
**A:** The programme fee includes tuition and development fees; university fees for registration and enrolment, cultural, sports, club and co-curricular activities; and fees for additional services such as contingency, outreach, placement, medical and counsellor services.

**Q:** What is the examination fee at LPU?
**A:** The examination fee is Rs. 5000 per semester. The examination fee for the 1st semester is payable along with the fee of the 2nd semester, or before.

**Q:** When do I pay the next semester's fee at LPU?
**A:** The fee for each following semester must be paid before the end of teaching of the preceding semester.

**Q:** I have a government scholarship or an education loan. Do I still pay LPU's fee on time?
**A:** Yes. LPU says students with post-matric, government or other scholarships or loans must still pay the programme fee, examination fee and facility charges before each semester starts. The university does not liaise with scholarship or loan providers.

**Q:** Is there an extra fee for the 2+2 international transfer option?
**A:** Yes. Students on the B.Tech CSE 2+2 international transfer option pay an additional International Transfer Fee of INR 50,000 with the 1st semester fee.

## 2. Scholarships at LPU

**Q:** What scholarship can I get through LPUNEST?
**A:** LPU's B.Tech CSE page lists LPUNEST scholarships of 50% of the programme fee for Category I, 40% for Category II, and 30% for Categories III and IV. The LPUNEST cut-off scores for each category differ between LPU's pages and are marked as tentative, so check the current cut-offs on the LPUNEST page.

**Q:** What other scholarships does LPU offer?
**A:** LPU's fee and scholarship page lists scholarships based on: percentage in the qualifying exam; rank or performance in national-level tests; toppers of school boards and higher-education institutions; innovation, startups and entrepreneurship; need-based financial aid; Rakshak Samman Anudan; orphans; persons with certain disabilities; and Shikshak Samman Anudan. LPU's CSE page also mentions sports, cultural and co-curricular scholarships. Amounts for these are not given on those pages.

**Q:** Are there full fee waivers at LPU?
**A:** LPU's prospectus mentions full-fee-waiver schemes for orphan candidates, candidates with certain disabilities, and toppers of central and state boards. A security fee of Rs 15,000 may be charged from applicants admitted under such waiver schemes.

**Q:** Can my LPU scholarship be cancelled?
**A:** Yes. If an applicant cannot prove eligibility or produce original documents by the deadline, they are treated as ineligible and any scholarship granted is cancelled, with no claim against the university. The scholarship amount also depends on the date of provisional registration.

**Q:** What happens to my scholarship if LPU reduces its fee?
**A:** If the programme fee is reduced, scholarship amounts in all brackets are reduced proportionately, and the fee after scholarship stays the same.

**Q:** Is someone from LPU allowed to ask me to pay into a personal account?
**A:** No. LPU warns that no LPU representative will ask for funds to be sent to a personal account or contact aspirants through personal chats.

## 3. Refund policy for Indian applicants (admission on or before 15 July 2026)

**Q:** What is LPU's refund policy if I withdraw?
**A:** Refund requests are accepted until 15 September 2026. Refunds are calculated on the full programme fee without scholarship, and the seat must be filled by a waitlisted applicant, if any. The deduction depends on the withdrawal date:

| Withdrawal date (2026) | Deduction |
|---|---|
| On or before 15 July | Processing fee of Rs 1000 |
| 16 to 31 July | Rs 2500 |
| 1 to 15 August | 10% of the fee |
| 16 to 31 August | 20% of the fee |
| 1 to 15 September | 50% of the fee |

**Q:** Are hostel and mess charges refunded at LPU?
**A:** The 10%, 20% and 50% deductions also apply to residential, mess, laundry and gym charges, calculated on the annual amount. For hostel, food, laundry and gym refunds requested up to 31 July 2026, the deduction is halved. For transport, a processing fee of Rs 500 applies if withdrawn on or before 15 August 2026.

**Q:** Is the LPUNEST fee refundable?
**A:** No. The LPUNEST application fee is non-refundable.
