# LPU Education Loan Assistance and Financing Policy

How Lovely Professional University (LPU) assists students with education loans through banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and EMI financing options, from LPU's official Education Loan Assistance page.

## 1. Student Loan Assistance Cell and financing options

**Q:** Does LPU have an office for education loan help?
**A:** Yes. LPU operates a Student Loan Assistance Cell, reachable at `+91-1824-444583` and `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in` (Lovely Professional University, Jalandhar-Delhi G.T. Road, Phagwara, Punjab 144411).

**Q:** What education loan and financing options are available through LPU's partners?
**A:** LPU facilitates three channels:
- **Education loans through banks:** LPU has tie-ups with public sector, private sector and regional rural banks (including State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, Canara Bank, IDBI Bank, Indian Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, Karur Vysya Bank, Assam Gramin Vikash Bank, Tripura Gramin Bank and J & K Grameen Bank). For loan amounts above Rs 7.5 lakhs, banks may require collateral security.
- **Education loans through NBFCs:** LPU has active tie-ups with leading NBFCs offering collateral-free education loans up to Rs 30 lakhs; a co-borrower is mandatory.
- **EMI and private financing:** Simplified EMI-based financing options through private partners.

## 2. Documents required by banks

The LPU Education Loan Assistance page lists the following documents commonly required by banks:
- Admission letter of the university showing fee, boarding and lodging expenses, plus any scholarship confirmation letter.
- Attested copies of proof of age/date of birth and residential address.
- Passport-size photographs of the applicant, co-obligants and guarantors.
- Copy of the PAN card of the student and parent/borrower.
- Marksheets and degree certificates of previous academic qualifications.
- Income proof or latest Income Tax Return (ITR) of parents/co-obligants/guarantors, if any.
- Bank account statements of the applicant/co-obligants/guarantors for the last six months, if any.
- Collateral security details with a Government-approved valuer's certificate (if applicable, for loan amounts above Rs 4.00 lakhs).

## 3. How to request a Loan Letter (offer letter) from LPU

**Q:** How do I get an LPU Loan Letter for my bank?
**A:** After completing provisional admission formalities, a student can request a fee structure and Loan Letter by filling out the Application Form for Loan Letter (obtainable from LPU campus, designated admission/camp offices, or by emailing Name and Admission ID to `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in`) and submitting the signed form in person to the Student Loan Assistance Cell, by post/courier, or as a scanned copy by email to `studentloanassistance@lpu.co.in`.

## 4. Eligibility, liability and regulatory guidelines

**Q:** Can continuing students apply for an education loan, and is LPU responsible for loan approval?
**A:** Students can apply for an education loan in the first year as well as in subsequent years of their programme. However, students and their parents/guardians are personally responsible for loan sanction, repayment and all liabilities; LPU does not guarantee loan approval, interest rates or terms, which depend solely on the respective bank or NBFC.

**Q:** Do banks need AICTE approval letters for LPU degree loans?
**A:** LPU cites the Supreme Court of India judgment in *Bharathidasan University and another vs AICTE and others* (Civil Appeal No. 2056 of 1999, dated 24 September 2001), which held that a university does not need prior AICTE approval to conduct technology programmes, so banks should not demand separate AICTE approval for university education loans.
