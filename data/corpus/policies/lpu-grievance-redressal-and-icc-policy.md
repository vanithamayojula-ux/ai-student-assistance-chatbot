# LPU Grievance Redressal, Ombudsperson and Internal Complaints Committee Policy

How students at Lovely Professional University (LPU) can submit academic, examination or fee grievances, escalate appeals to the Ombudsperson, or file complaints with the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), from LPU's official Student Grievance Redressal page and ICC disclosures.

## 1. Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC) and Ombudsperson

**Q:** Who handles student grievances at LPU?
**A:** LPU has a Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC) and a University Ombudsperson. According to LPU's official grievance redressal page, the SGRC is chaired by Dr. Lovi Raj Gupta (Pro Vice Chancellor and Executive Dean), with Dr. Sorabh Lakhanpal (Executive Dean), Dr. Amit Dutt (Dean), Dr. Tushinder Preet Kaur (Assistant Dean) and Dr. Suresh Mani (Associate Dean) as members, and Mr. Boppudi Yuva Kumar as Student Special Invitee. The University Ombudsperson is Dr. C.L. Chandan.

**Q:** How do I submit a grievance at LPU?
**A:** Students can submit a grievance online through LPU's Student Grievance Redressal page (`https://www.lpu.in/student-grievance-redressal.php`) using OTP email verification. The form accepts grievances under four categories: `Examination`, `Academic`, `Fees`, and `Others`, with a subject, description and supporting attachment. Students can also raise day-to-day service requests via the Relationship Management System (RMS) on UMS, or write to the Registrar at `registrar@lpu.co.in` (landline `+91-1824-510274`, Registrar mobile `+91-9915020408`).

## 2. Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

**Q:** How does LPU handle complaints of sexual harassment?
**A:** LPU maintains an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) constituted under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the UGC (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal of Sexual Harassment of Women Employees and Students in Higher Educational Institutions) Regulations, 2015.

**Q:** Who is the Presiding Officer of LPU's ICC and how do I contact the committee?
**A:** The Presiding Officer of LPU's Internal Complaints Committee is Dr. Neeta Raj Sharma (Professor), reachable at `neeta.raj@lpu.co.in` and `+91-8283921144`. Sexual harassment complaints can be registered directly at `icc@lpu.co.in`. According to LPU's published ICC composition, the 9-member committee includes faculty and administrative members (Col. Rajendra Kumar Gupta, Dr. Sunaina Ahuja, Ms. Neetu Sarup and Member-Secretary Mr. Navdeep Singh Thind), an external NGO member (Dr. Shaffali Lakhanpal) and three student representatives.

## 3. Student well-being and crisis response contact

**Q:** Who is the Nodal Officer for Student Well-Being and Crisis Response at LPU?
**A:** LPU lists Dr. Vishal Sharma as Nodal Officer for Student Well-Being, Crisis Response and Compliance, reachable at `dsr@lpu.co.in` and `+91-1824-520150`.
