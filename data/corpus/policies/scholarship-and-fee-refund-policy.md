# Scholarship and Fee Refund Policy

This policy sets out the scholarships available to B.Tech students of Sutlej Valley University (SVU) in 2026-27, the conditions for keeping them, and the rules for refund of fees when a student withdraws from the university.

## 1. Entrance Merit Waiver for new students

The Entrance Merit Waiver is a reduction in tuition fee given to first-year B.Tech students on the basis of their JEE Main percentile. It is applied automatically at admission and does not need a separate application.

| JEE Main percentile | Waiver on tuition fee |
|---|---|
| 98 and above | 50% |
| 95 to 97.99 | 30% |
| 90 to 94.99 | 15% |

The waiver applies only to the tuition fee of Rs. 1,85,000 per year and not to the development and amenities fee, hostel fee or mess fee. Students admitted through the SVU Entrance Test are considered for the waiver on the equivalent percentile published with the merit list.

## 2. Conditions for continuing the merit waiver

A student who holds an Entrance Merit Waiver must keep the waiver by meeting all of the following conditions at the end of every academic year: a CGPA of at least 8.0, attendance of at least 75% in all courses, and no active backlog or disciplinary action.

If a student fails to meet the conditions, the waiver is withdrawn from the next year and the full tuition fee is payable. The waiver can be restored once, for the following year, if the student's CGPA is 8.0 or above in the next year. Students on a break of study do not receive the waiver for the period of the break.

## 3. Merit-cum-Means Grant

The Merit-cum-Means Grant is a need-based grant for continuing students. It amounts to Rs. 40,000 per academic year and is paid as Rs. 20,000 credited against each semester fee.

To be eligible, the annual family income must be below Rs. 4,00,000, and the student must have a CGPA of at least 7.0 and attendance of at least 75%. A student who already receives an Entrance Merit Waiver of 30% or more cannot hold this grant at the same time. Applications are invited once a year through a notice, and the Scholarship Committee chaired by the Dean Student Affairs decides on the awards. Contact scholarships@svu.example for details.

## 4. Sports Excellence Scholarship

SVU offers a Sports Excellence Scholarship to students who have won medals in recognised competitions. A student who has won a medal at the national level receives a waiver of up to 25% of the tuition fee. A student who has won a medal at the state level receives a waiver of 10% of the tuition fee.

The scholarship is given on the recommendation of the Sports Officer after trials held in the second week of August. To continue, the student must represent SVU in at least 3 inter-university events a year, keep attendance at 75% and maintain a CGPA of at least 6.0. The sports scholarship and the Merit-cum-Means Grant cannot be combined.

## 5. Refund of fees on withdrawal

A student who withdraws from the university or cancels the admission may get a refund of the semester academic fee (tuition and development fee) as per the table below. The percentage depends on the date on which the written withdrawal application is received by the Accounts Office.

| Date of receipt of withdrawal application | Refund of semester academic fee |
|---|---|
| 30 days or more before classes begin | 100% less Rs. 5,000 processing charge |
| 1 to 29 days before classes begin | 90% |
| Up to 15 days after classes begin | 70% |
| 16 to 30 days after classes begin | 50% |
| More than 30 days after classes begin | No refund |

## 6. Hostel, mess and security deposit refunds

The hostel fee for a semester is not refunded once the semester has started. If a student vacates the hostel permanently after giving 15 days' written notice to the Chief Warden, the mess charges for the unused full months are refunded at Rs. 4,500 per month.

The hostel security deposit of Rs. 10,000 is refunded within 30 days of the student vacating the room, after deducting charges for any damage or missing items. The student must first obtain a no-dues certificate from the hostel, the library and the Accounts Office. The refund is made to the same bank account from which the deposit was paid.

## 7. Procedure and time for refunds

To claim a refund, the student must submit the withdrawal form with the fee receipt, the original ID card and the no-dues certificates to the Accounts Office, Block B, Room B-12. For a minor student, the form must be signed by a parent.

The refund is processed within 21 working days of the approval, and it is transferred only to the bank account from which the fee was originally paid, not to cash. Excess payments and scholarships sanctioned after the fee payment are also refunded within 21 working days. Queries on refunds can be sent to accounts@svu.example.

## 8. Fees that are never refundable

Some charges are not refunded in any case. These are the application fee of Rs. 1,500, the one-time registration fee of Rs. 10,000, the examination fee, the late fee, the processing fee of Rs. 1,000 for the instalment facility, and the condonation fee for attendance shortage.

A student who is expelled or suspended for a disciplinary reason, including unfair means or ragging, is not eligible for any refund of academic, hostel or mess fees. The transport fee is refunded in proportion to the unused months only if the bus pass is surrendered at the Transport Office.
