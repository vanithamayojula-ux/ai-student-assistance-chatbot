# Academic Progression and Degree Requirements

This document explains how B.Tech students at Sutlej Valley University (SVU) move from one year to the next, how many credits they must earn, and how long they may take to finish the degree. It applies to the 2026-27 academic year.

## 1. Duration of the programme

The B.Tech programme at SVU has a normal duration of 4 years, which is 8 semesters. A student who needs more time because of backlogs, a break of study or repeated courses may take longer, but the maximum duration allowed to complete the degree is 6 years, which is 12 semesters, counted from the date of first admission.

The period of any approved break of study is included in the maximum duration of 6 years. A student who does not complete the degree within 6 years loses the registration and cannot continue in the programme, but may be given a certificate for the credits earned on request.

## 2. Credit requirement for the degree

To be awarded the B.Tech CSE degree, a student must earn all 160 credits listed in the programme structure and must have a final CGPA of at least 5.0. Credits are earned only in courses where the student gets grade P or better, so courses with grade F, AB or DT do not count.

In addition, the student must complete the summer internship of at least 6 weeks, submit the major project report, and clear all dues of the university, the library and the hostel. A no-dues certificate from each of these offices is required before the final grade card and the provisional certificate are issued.

## 3. Promotion from year to year

A student is promoted to a higher year only if a minimum number of credits has been earned from the earlier semesters. The rule is as follows:

| To enter | Credits required to be earned |
|---|---|
| Semester 3 | 26 out of 42 credits of Semesters 1 and 2 |
| Semester 5 | 52 out of 86 credits of Semesters 1 to 4 |
| Semester 7 | 78 out of 129 credits of Semesters 1 to 6 |

These thresholds are about 60% of the credits of the earlier semesters. A student who does not meet the threshold is not promoted and must clear enough backlogs before being allowed to register in the higher semester.

## 4. Credit load per semester

The regular credit load of a semester is the credit value given in the programme structure, which is between 15 and 22 credits. A student may register for a minimum of 16 credits and a maximum of 26 credits in a semester, including backlog courses being repeated.

Registration for more than the regular load, up to 26 credits, needs approval from the Dean Academics and is allowed only if the student's CGPA is 7.0 or above. A student whose CGPA is below 5.0 is not permitted to take more than the regular load. Course timetable clashes are not an acceptable reason for dropping a course.

## 5. Academic probation

A student whose SGPA is below 5.0 in a semester is placed on academic warning, and the parents are informed in writing. A student who has an SGPA below 5.0 in two consecutive semesters, or a CGPA below 5.0 at the end of any semester from Semester 4 onwards, is placed on academic probation.

A student on probation must meet the faculty mentor every 2 weeks, may not hold any office in student clubs, and must register for a reduced load of at most 18 credits. Probation is lifted when the SGPA of the next semester is 5.0 or above and the CGPA is at least 5.0.

## 6. Course registration and add/drop

Students register for all courses of a semester online through the student portal in the registration week before classes begin. Registration is allowed only after the semester fee has been paid or an approved instalment plan is in place.

The add/drop window is open for the first two weeks of the semester. For the odd semester of 2026-27, classes start on 3 August 2026 and add/drop closes on 14 August 2026. After the window closes, no course can be added, and a course dropped later is recorded with the grade W without grade points and without refund of any fee.

## 7. Break of study and withdrawal

A student may apply for a break of study of one semester or up to two semesters (one year) for reasons such as serious illness, family emergency, an entrepreneurship venture or an overseas exchange. The application must be approved in advance by the Dean Academics, on the recommendation of the Head of Department.

A break is not permitted in the first semester, and the total break cannot exceed 2 semesters during the programme. The break period counts within the 6-year maximum duration. A student who wishes to leave the university permanently must apply for withdrawal and clear all dues, and the refund rules of the Scholarship and Fee Refund Policy apply.

## 8. Repeating a year and lateral entry

A student who is not promoted at the end of a year may repeat the failed semesters with the next batch, attending classes afresh. The grades from the earlier attempt are replaced by the new grades, but both attempts count in the limit of 4 attempts per course.

Lateral entry into Semester 3 is available for diploma holders who have scored at least 60% in a 3-year engineering diploma. Lateral entry students need to earn 118 credits in Semesters 3 to 8 to complete the degree, and they must clear the bridge courses in Mathematics and Programming in their first year.
