# Attendance Regulations

These regulations set out the attendance rules that apply to all B.Tech students of Sutlej Valley University (SVU) from the academic year 2026-27. They explain how attendance is counted, when shortage can be condoned and what happens to students who fall short.

## 1. Minimum attendance requirement

Every B.Tech student at SVU must have at least 75% attendance in each course in a semester in order to be eligible to appear in the end-term examination of that course. The percentage is calculated course by course, not as an overall average across all courses.

Attendance is calculated as the number of classes attended divided by the total number of classes held for that course from the first day of the semester up to the last day of classes, multiplied by 100. A student who meets the 75% requirement in some courses but not in others is eligible to appear only in the courses where the requirement is met, unless the shortage is condoned under these regulations.

## 2. Condonation of shortage

Students whose attendance in a course is 65% or more but below 75% may apply for condonation of the shortage. Condonation is granted by the Dean Academics only on genuine grounds, which are a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner or leave that was approved in advance under the Leave and Grievance Policy.

A condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course must be paid at the Accounts Office. A student can use condonation in at most 2 semesters during the entire programme. The application must be submitted to the Dean Academics office, Block A, Room A-110, at least 7 days before the start of the end-term examinations, along with supporting documents.

## 3. Attendance below 65%: no condonation

If a student's attendance in a course is below 65%, the shortage cannot be condoned under any circumstances, and no medical certificate will change this. The student is declared detained in that course and receives the grade DT, which carries zero grade points.

A student with the DT grade is not allowed to appear in the end-term examination of that course, and the hall ticket for that course is not issued. The student must repeat the course by attending classes afresh the next time it is offered, and must register for it again and pay the applicable fee. The DT grade stays on the grade card until the course is cleared.

## 4. How attendance is counted

Lectures, tutorials and laboratory sessions are all counted together as classes for a course. For courses with a laboratory part, such as Data Structures (CS201), the lab sessions are included in the single attendance percentage of that course.

A student who enters a class more than 10 minutes after it has started is marked absent for that class. Attendance is marked by the course instructor on the student portal, and proxy attendance is treated as unfair means under the Student Conduct Regulations. Classes cancelled by the university are not counted in the total number of classes held.

## 5. Duty leave for university activities

Students who represent SVU in sports, cultural, technical or academic events, such as inter-university tournaments or hackathons, may be granted duty leave. Prior written approval from the Dean Student Affairs, Block A, Room A-118, is required before the student leaves the campus.

Days of approved duty leave are counted as present, up to a maximum of 10% of the total classes of a course in a semester. Duty leave beyond that limit is treated as ordinary absence. The student must submit the participation certificate to the Dean Student Affairs office within 5 working days of returning to campus, otherwise the duty leave is cancelled.

## 6. Display of attendance and correction of errors

Attendance for every course is updated on the student portal every Friday by 6:00 pm. Students are expected to check it regularly. The Department also sends a warning email to the student and parents when attendance in any course drops below 80%.

If a student finds an error, the student should first inform the course instructor in writing within 5 working days of the figure appearing on the portal. If the matter is not resolved, the student may write to the Dean Academics at deanacademics@svu.example within 3 working days after that. After these periods, the portal figures are treated as final.

## 7. Medical absence and attendance

Absence due to illness is counted as absence in the attendance record. A medical certificate does not automatically add attendance, but it makes the student eligible to apply for condonation if attendance falls between 65% and 75%.

The medical certificate must be submitted to the Dean Academics office within 7 days of the student rejoining classes. Certificates for hospitalisation must include the discharge summary. Certificates from relatives who are doctors are not accepted. The detailed procedure for medical leave is given in the Leave, Medical Leave and Grievance Redressal Policy.
