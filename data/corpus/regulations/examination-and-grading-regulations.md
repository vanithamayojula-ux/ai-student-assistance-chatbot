# Examination and Grading Regulations

These regulations describe how courses are assessed and graded for B.Tech students at Sutlej Valley University (SVU) in 2026-27. They cover marks distribution, pass criteria, the grade table, CGPA calculation, backlogs and re-evaluation.

## 1. Assessment components

For a theory-only course, the total of 100 marks is split into 40 marks of continuous internal assessment and 60 marks of end-term examination. The 40 internal marks consist of a mid-term examination of 20 marks, quizzes of 10 marks and assignments of 10 marks.

Courses that have a laboratory part follow the scheme given in their own syllabus. For example, Data Structures (CS201) gives 20 marks to the mid-term, 10 to quizzes, 20 to laboratory work and 50 to the end-term. The mid-term examination lasts 90 minutes and the end-term examination lasts 3 hours for a full course. Internal marks are displayed on the student portal within 7 days of each assessment.

## 2. Passing criteria

To pass a course, a student must score at least 40 marks out of 100 in total, and at least 40% of the maximum marks of the end-term examination. For a theory course with a 60-mark end-term, this means a minimum of 24 marks in the end-term. For a course with a 50-mark end-term, it means a minimum of 20 marks.

A student who scores 40 or more overall but is below the end-term minimum is declared failed and gets the grade F. A student who is absent from the end-term examination without approval is given the grade AB, which is treated as a fail until the course is cleared in a reappear examination.

## 3. Grade table

SVU uses a 10-point grading scale, and marks are converted into letter grades as shown below.

| Marks out of 100 | Grade | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| 90 to 100 | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80 to 89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70 to 79 | A | 8 |
| 60 to 69 | B+ | 7 |
| 50 to 59 | B | 6 |
| 45 to 49 | C | 5 |
| 40 to 44 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| Below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |

Grades DT (detained for shortage of attendance) and AB (absent) also carry 0 grade points.

## 4. SGPA, CGPA and percentage

The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the sum of (credits of each course multiplied by its grade points) divided by the total credits registered in the semester. The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated the same way over all courses of all semesters completed so far. Courses with grade F, AB or DT are included in the calculation with 0 grade points until they are cleared.

For example, a student with grade points 9 in a 4-credit course and 8 in a 3-credit course has a total of 36 + 24 = 60 over 7 credits, giving 8.57. To convert CGPA into an approximate percentage, multiply the CGPA by 9.5.

## 5. Backlogs and reappear examinations

A course in which a student has received F, AB or DT (after repeating) is called a backlog. A student can appear in a reappear examination for a backlog course in the next end-term cycle in which that course is scheduled, so odd-semester courses are held in December and even-semester courses in May.

The reappear fee is Rs. 1,000 per course. A student has a maximum of 4 attempts for any course, which is the first attempt plus 3 reappear attempts. Internal marks earned earlier are carried forward. Students of the final year with at most 2 backlogs can also appear in the Special Backlog Examination held in July, so that they do not lose a year.

## 6. Re-evaluation of answer scripts

A student who is not satisfied with the marks in an end-term examination may first request a photocopy of the answer script by paying Rs. 500 per course within 5 days of the result declaration. A request for re-evaluation must be made on the student portal within 7 days of the result declaration, with a fee of Rs. 1,500 per course.

The script is checked by a different examiner. If the revised marks differ from the original by less than 10 marks, the higher marks are awarded. If the difference is 10 marks or more, a third examiner evaluates the script and the average of the two nearest marks is awarded. Re-evaluation is not available for mid-term examinations, labs or projects.

## 7. Missed examinations and make-up

A student who misses a mid-term examination because of illness or hospitalisation may take a make-up examination if a medical certificate is submitted to the Dean Academics within 3 days of the missed paper. The make-up mid-term is held within 10 days of the original date. Only one make-up mid-term is allowed per course.

There is no make-up for the end-term examination. A student who misses it with a documented medical emergency can appear in the next reappear cycle as a first attempt, without the reappear fee, if the Controller of Examinations approves the request. Any other absence leads to the grade AB.

## 8. Degree classification

A student must have a CGPA of at least 5.0 and must have cleared all 160 credits to be awarded the B.Tech degree. The class is awarded on the final CGPA as follows:

- First Class with Distinction: CGPA of 8.0 or above, with all courses cleared in the first attempt.
- First Class: CGPA of 6.5 or above, but less than 8.0 (or 8.0 or above with a reappear attempt).
- Second Class: CGPA of 5.0 or above, but less than 6.5.

The degree certificate shows the final CGPA and the class, and the consolidated grade card is issued by the Controller of Examinations, coe@svu.example.
