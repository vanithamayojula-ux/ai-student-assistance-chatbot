# LPU Attendance, Examination and Grading Rules

Rules on attendance, examinations, grading, re-appear and re-evaluation at Lovely Professional University (LPU). The attendance rules come from LPU's official Prospectus 2026. LPU does not publish its full examination ordinance on its public website, so some details below come from posts on LPU's official student blog (happenings.lpu.in); each such point says so and gives its date. Students should confirm current rules on UMS (University Management System).

## 1. Minimum attendance at LPU

**Q:** What is the minimum attendance required at LPU?
**A:** According to LPU's Prospectus 2026, a student should attend 80% or more in aggregate of delivered classes, and a relaxation of 5% is granted for medical reasons and other genuine exigencies, so a minimum of 75% attendance in aggregate is mandatory at LPU.

**Q:** Which classes count towards LPU attendance?
**A:** The aggregate covers all registered theory courses (lectures and tutorials) and practical courses of the term, including optional, additional, elective, specialization and backlog courses.

**Q:** Is attendance relaxed at LPU if I joined late?
**A:** LPU's prospectus says the minimum attendance remains the same for late joiners and no relaxation is given for late joining. For students admitted after the session starts, attendance is counted from the date of admission.

**Q:** Is there a separate medical leave process at LPU?
**A:** The prospectus describes only the 5% relaxation for medical reasons and genuine exigencies. A separate condonation or medical-leave procedure is not published on LPU's public pages; check UMS or the school office.

**Q:** What happens if my attendance is short at LPU?
**A:** A student found ineligible to appear in an examination due to shortage of attendance is liable to vacate the LPU hostel, and LPU may bar students with attendance below the prescribed limit from applying for hostel. An LPU student blog post (January 2021) adds that a student who fails to maintain 75% attendance in a subject has to sign up for backlog classes in it.

**Q:** Do I get marks for attendance at LPU?
**A:** According to an LPU student blog post (January 2021), attendance carries 5 marks: more than 90% gets 5 marks, 85-90% gets 4, 80-85% gets 3, and 75-80% gets 2.

## 2. Examinations at LPU

**Q:** What examinations are held in an LPU term?
**A:** Each LPU term has a Mid Term Test (MTT) and an End Term Examination (ETE). LPU's assessment page says End-Term examinations are conducted after the end of every academic term. Dates for 2026-27 are in the LPU Academic Calendar document.

**Q:** How are marks divided between CA, mid-term and end-term at LPU?
**A:** LPU's official pages do not publish the weightage. An LPU student blog post (January 2021) gives this split for a theory course: attendance 5 marks, continuous assessment (best 2 of 3) 20 marks, mid-term examination 25 marks and end-term examination 50 marks, for 100 marks in total. Treat it as indicative; it may have changed.

**Q:** Can I see my answer sheet at LPU?
**A:** Yes. LPU's assessment page says students can examine their evaluated answer sheets in scheduled scrutiny sessions.

**Q:** What if I miss a test at LPU because I was on duty leave?
**A:** According to an LPU student blog post (October 2020), missed continuous assessment or mid-term marks are prorated from the other components you attempted. There is no proration for the end-term exam; an end-term exam missed on duty leave can be taken in the next term with no reappear fee.

## 3. Re-evaluation at LPU

**Q:** How do I apply for re-evaluation at LPU?
**A:** LPU's assessment page says that after a Mid Term Test or End Term Examination, students who are not satisfied with the evaluation can apply online through UMS for re-evaluation of their answer booklets. The re-evaluation fee and deadline are not published on LPU's public pages; check UMS.

## 4. Grading, TGPA and CGPA at LPU

**Q:** What grading system does LPU use?
**A:** According to an LPU student blog post (July 2018), LPU follows a relative grading system, benchmarked to the highest marks in the student's school. The grade points are: O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, D 4, and 0 for E (reappear), F (fail), G (backlog) and I (result incomplete).

**Q:** How are TGPA and CGPA calculated at LPU?
**A:** TGPA (term grade point average) is the sum of grade points times course credits, divided by total credits for the term. CGPA is calculated the same way over all terms so far; it is not the average of the TGPAs. (Source: LPU student blog post, July 2018.)

**Q:** How do I convert LPU CGPA to percentage?
**A:** For admission purposes, LPU's Prospectus 2026 uses a multiplying factor of 10 to convert CGPA to percentage.

## 5. Re-appear, backlog and grade improvement at LPU

**Q:** What happens if I fail a course at LPU?
**A:** LPU charges a reappear or make-up examination fee and fees for backlog courses, as listed in its prospectus; the amounts are not published there. According to an LPU student blog post (January 2021), a student who cannot clear a subject in 5 reappear attempts must sign up for backlog classes in it.

**Q:** Can I improve my grade in a course I have passed at LPU?
**A:** According to the same LPU student blog post (January 2021), a passed subject can be re-attempted up to 5 times to improve its grade.

**Q:** Do backlogs affect placements at LPU?
**A:** LPU's prospectus says the university may set conditions such as a specific CGPA and/or a maximum number of permissible backlogs or re-appears for taking part in placement activities.
