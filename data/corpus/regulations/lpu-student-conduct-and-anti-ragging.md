# LPU Student Conduct, Discipline and Anti-Ragging Rules

Rules on ragging, discipline and misconduct at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official Prospectus 2026 and its anti-ragging page.

## 1. Ragging at LPU

**Q:** Is ragging allowed at LPU?
**A:** No. LPU's prospectus states that ragging is totally prohibited in the University. Anyone found guilty of ragging or of abetting it is liable to be punished under the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009, and under any penal law in force.

**Q:** Do LPU students have to give an anti-ragging undertaking?
**A:** Yes. Under a UGC letter dated 16 September 2022 (D.O.No.F.1-15/2021(ARC)), every LPU student and their parent or guardian must submit an online anti-ragging undertaking every academic year at www.antiragging.in.

**Q:** How do I report ragging?
**A:** LPU's anti-ragging page lists the national anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522 (24x7, toll free) and the email helpline@antiragging.in. The same page lists LPU's Anti-Ragging Committee and Anti-Ragging Squad contacts.

## 2. Prohibited conduct at LPU

**Q:** What is prohibited on the LPU campus?
**A:** LPU's prospectus says ragging, hooliganism, misconduct, gambling, smoking, liquor or alcohol, drugs, explosives and weapons are strictly prohibited within and outside the precincts of the University. Students found guilty face severe punishment and may be reported to the police if the University decides.

**Q:** What counts as disciplinary misconduct at LPU?
**A:** LPU's prospectus lists, among others: physical assault; carrying weapons or intoxicants; discrimination on the basis of caste, creed or language; irregular attendance or negligence towards classes, tests or examinations; protests, strikes or mass cuts; bribery; damaging property; disrupting tests or examinations; and collecting money without written approval.

**Q:** Can misconduct affect my LPU hostel?
**A:** Yes. A student detained for disciplinary misconduct is liable to vacate the hostel, and students with pending disciplinary cases may be debarred from the hostel.
