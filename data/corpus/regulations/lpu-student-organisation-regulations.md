# LPU Student Organisation Rules and Registration Guidelines

Rules for joining, promoting and registering student organisations and clubs at Lovely Professional University (LPU), from LPU's official Student Organisations page.

## 1. Scale of student organisations

**Q:** How many student organisations does LPU have?
**A:** LPU's Student Organisations page reports 350+ student organisations across 18+ categories, with 10,000+ student members and 500+ events held each year.

## 2. Membership, promoter and facilitator rules

**Q:** Is there any membership fee to join a student organisation at LPU?
**A:** No. LPU states that no membership fee is charged from students for joining a Student Organization. However, members may be asked for contributions during fundraising for a particular event if that fundraising mechanism is specified in the organisation's constitution.

**Q:** Who can start or promote a new student organisation at LPU?
**A:** Students from the 2nd year onwards with a CGPA of at least 7.0 can act as a promoter, and a student may promote only one student organisation in a year. Every student organisation must also have a Faculty Facilitator (a faculty or staff member not below the rank of Lecturer or equivalent) acting as mentor.

**Q:** Can I join more than one student organisation at LPU?
**A:** Yes. Students from the 2nd year onwards can become members of more than one student organisation, but can serve on the Organization Board of only one Student Organization.

## 3. Do's, don'ts and UMS registration pathway

**Q:** What activities are prohibited for LPU student organisations?
**A:** Student organisations are prohibited from engaging in any political, semi-political or religious activities that could harm the moral values, cultural ethic or reputation of the university. Their objectives must not humiliate or disgrace any community, individual or group on the basis of race, ancestry, place of origin, colour, ethnic origin, disability, age, caste, creed, gender or religion, or promote a controversial agenda, and they must not act against the student code of conduct and discipline.

**Q:** How do I register a new student organisation or read the full manual at LPU?
**A:** On UMS, follow `UMS Navigation -> Student Welfare System (SWS) -> Student Organization Registration` to register a new organisation. To read the Department of Student Organization Manual 2023, follow `UMS Navigation -> Student Welfare System (SWS) -> Policies -> Department Wise -> Student Organization Cell -> Policy Wise -> Department of Student Organization Manual 2023`.
