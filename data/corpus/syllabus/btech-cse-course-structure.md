# B.Tech CSE Course Structure 2026-27

This document gives the semester-wise course structure of the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) programme at Sutlej Valley University (SVU) for the 2026-27 batch and curriculum. Course codes, L-T-P hours and credits are listed for every semester.

## 1. Programme overview and credit distribution

The B.Tech CSE programme at SVU has eight semesters and a total of 160 credits. The credit value of a course is calculated as L + T + P/2, where L is lecture hours, T is tutorial hours and P is practical hours per week. The programme is run by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Block C, Room C-101.

Credits by category are: basic sciences and mathematics 24, humanities 12, engineering fundamentals 20, core computer science 60, projects and internship 17, and electives 27 (18 programme elective credits and 9 open elective credits). Semester-wise credits are 21, 21, 22, 22, 22, 21, 16 and 15 for Semesters 1 to 8 respectively. A student must earn all 160 credits to be awarded the degree.

## 9. Semester 8 courses

Semester 8 carries 15 credits, the lightest load in the programme, so that students can focus on the major project or a full-semester industry placement.

| Code | Course | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| CS451 | Major Project Phase II | 10 |
| PE-VI | Programme Elective VI | 3 |

Major Project Phase II (CS451) is worth 10 credits, the largest single course in the programme. Students on an approved industry placement in Semester 8 are evaluated through a joint report from the company mentor and the SVU faculty guide.

## 10. Electives, internship and project rules

Programme electives are chosen from a pool announced by the department before registration. The pool for 2026-27 includes Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Blockchain Technology, Internet of Things, Cyber Forensics and Distributed Systems. An elective runs only if at least 20 students register for it.

Open electives are offered by other departments and must be taken from outside the CSE department. The summer internship lasts a minimum of 6 weeks and must be completed between Semester 6 and Semester 7. The internship report is submitted within 15 days of the start of Semester 7. The major project is done in teams of at most 4 students under a faculty guide from the CSE department.
