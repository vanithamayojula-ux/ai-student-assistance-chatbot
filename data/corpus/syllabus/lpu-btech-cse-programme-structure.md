# LPU B.Tech CSE Programme Structure and Specialisations

How the B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is organised at Lovely Professional University (LPU): duration, the kinds of courses, the subjects in each year, specialisations, and projects and internships. The semester-wise list is LPU's own sample curriculum, published on LPU's official blog, which says the curriculum "is subject to changes and/or review as and when prescribed by the University". LPU does not publish total credits or per-course syllabi on its public pages.

## 1. Duration and course types

**Q:** How long is the B.Tech CSE programme at LPU?
**A:** LPU's B.Tech CSE is 4 years, divided into 8 semesters (terms).

**Q:** What kinds of courses are there in LPU B.Tech CSE?
**A:** LPU's programme page lists these curriculum components: Foundation and Core Courses, Engineering Minors, Open Minor, Industry Interface, Study Abroad, Placement Pathways, Specialisation and Electives, Discipline Minor, Generic Elective and Undergraduate Research. Industry certifications, MOOCs, hackathons and competitive events, workshops, and live, incubation, community and NGO projects are described as an integral part of the curriculum.

**Q:** Which language electives does LPU B.Tech CSE offer?
**A:** French, German, Japanese, Spanish and Communication Skills.

## 2. Subjects in Year 1

- Autumn term: Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering (with lab), Engineering Physics, Internet Programming Laboratory, Mathematics for Engineers, Orientation to Computing-I, Python Programming.
- Spring term: Computer Programming, Database Management Systems, Differential Equations and Vector Calculus, Engineering Drawing with AutoCAD, Environmental Studies, Language Elective 1, Orientation to Computing-II, Software Engineering.

## 3. Subjects in Year 2

- Autumn term: Community Development Project, Computer Networks, Data Structures and Algorithms, Design Thinking, Discrete Mathematics, Internetworking Essentials, Language Elective 2, Object Oriented Programming, Virtualisation and Cloud Computing.
- Spring term: Aptitude Elective 1, Artificial Intelligence Essentials, Computer Organisation and Design, Engineering Minor Elective 1, Operating Systems (with lab), Probability and Statistics, Programming in Java.

## 4. Subjects in Year 3

- Autumn term: Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Engineering Minor Electives 2 and 3, Industry Ethics and Legal Issues, Open Minor 1, Pathway Electives 1 and 2, Training Elective 1.
- Spring term: Engineering Minor Electives 4 and 5, Formal Languages and Automation Theory, Open Minor 2, Pathway Electives 3 and 4.

## 5. Subjects in Year 4

- Autumn term: Capstone Project-I, Engineering Minor Elective 6, Foundations of Indian Knowledge Systems, Industry Co-op Project-I, Online Academic Course, Open Minor 3.
- Spring term: Capstone Project-II, Comprehensive Seminar, Department Elective 1 (with lab), Open Minor 4, Training Elective 2.

Department electives listed by LPU include Advanced Java Programming, Blockchain, Network Security and Cryptography, Game Development in 3D, and Linux System Administration.

## 6. Specialisations and variants of B.Tech CSE at LPU

**Q:** What specialisations does LPU offer in B.Tech CSE?
**A:** LPU offers B.Tech CSE with Hons. specialisations in Data Science and Data Engineering, Cyber Security and Blockchain, and Full Stack Software Development, and with industry-collaborative specialisations such as IoT and AI Analytics with Intel, Cloud Computing and Gen AI with Google Cloud, Gen AI with Microsoft, Fintech and AI with Paytm, AR and VR, AI-Powered DevOps, Data Science with ML and UX/UI. LPU also offers specialised B.Tech degrees in AI and Machine Learning, AI and Data Science, Robotics and AI, and Computer Science and Business Systems. The full list of variants from LPU's blog is below.

- Hons. (industry collaborative): Data Science and Data Engineering; Cyber Security and Blockchain; Full Stack Software Development.
- Industry collaborative, with partner: IoT and AI Analytics (Intel); Cloud Computing and Gen AI (Google Cloud); Fintech and AI (Paytm); AR and VR (Bharatverse); AI-Powered DevOps (Xebia); Gen AI (Microsoft); Data Science with ML (upGrad); UX/UI (ImaginXP).
- Work integrated: AI and Data Analytics; AI and Data Engineering; Software Product Engineering.
- Specialised degrees: Computer Science and Business Systems; AI and Data Science; Robotics and AI; AI and Machine Learning.
- Immersion: CSE - AI and System Design with Global Tech Immersion.
- Integrated: B.Tech CSE with MBA; B.Tech CSE with an M.Sc in Economics.
- International credit transfer: CSE 2+2 with an international credit transfer option.
- Lateral entry after a diploma: CSE; IT; AI and Machine Learning.

## 7. Projects, internships and training in LPU B.Tech CSE

**Q:** When do LPU CSE students do projects?
**A:** In LPU's sample curriculum, Capstone Project-I and Industry Co-op Project-I are in the Year 4 Autumn term, and Capstone Project-II and a Comprehensive Seminar are in the Year 4 Spring term. A Community Development Project is in Year 2. LPU's public pages do not give the credits, group size or assessment rules for these projects.

**Q:** What are training electives in LPU B.Tech CSE?
**A:** Training electives are practical, industry-oriented components. LPU lists Industry Co-op Project II, Industry Internship Project, Seminar on Summer Training, and Training in Programming. Training Elective 1 is in Year 3 Autumn and Training Elective 2 in Year 4 Spring.

**Q:** Is an internship compulsory in LPU B.Tech CSE, and how long is it?
**A:** LPU's public pages do not state general internship rules or credits for regular B.Tech CSE. For the B.Tech (Hons.) Full Stack programme, LPU mentions a 4 to 6 week internship after the 2nd year and a 4 to 6 month internship in the 4th year; this applies to that programme only.
