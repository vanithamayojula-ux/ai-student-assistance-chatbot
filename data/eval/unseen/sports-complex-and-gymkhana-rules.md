# Sports Complex and Gymkhana Rules

These rules apply to every student who uses the Sports Complex of Sutlej Valley University (SVU). The document was written only to test the local answer model: it is not part of the corpus and no training question was written from it.

## 1. Timings and entry

The Sports Complex is open from 5:30 AM to 9:30 PM on all days except national holidays. The indoor badminton hall closes earlier, at 8:45 PM, so that the wooden floor can be cleaned. Entry is allowed only with a valid student ID card, which is scanned at the turnstile near Gate 3.

A student who forgets the ID card may enter at most 2 times in a semester by signing the register kept with the duty supervisor. Guests are not allowed inside the complex except on the annual sports day.

## 2. Gymnasium membership

The gymnasium is free for hostel residents. Day scholars pay a membership fee of Rs. 1,200 per semester at the Accounts Office and show the receipt to the Physical Education Office, Sports Complex, Room S-04. Membership is activated within 2 working days.

Every new member must attend one orientation session with the gym instructor before using the weights area. Orientation sessions are held every Monday and Thursday at 6:00 PM. The gymnasium admits at most 40 students at a time, and each session is limited to 75 minutes during the evening rush between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM.

## 3. Booking courts and equipment

Badminton, squash and tennis courts are booked on the student portal under the Sports tab. A slot is 45 minutes long and a student can hold at most 3 bookings in a week. A booking can be cancelled without penalty up to 2 hours before the slot; a student who does not turn up for a booked slot 3 times in a semester loses the booking facility for 30 days.

Rackets, balls and other equipment are issued from the equipment counter against the student ID card and must be returned the same day by 9:00 PM. A late return is fined Rs. 50 per day, and the cost of lost or damaged equipment is recovered from the student.

## 4. Swimming pool

The swimming pool is open from 1 March to 31 October and stays closed in the winter months. Students must pass a swimming test conducted by the lifeguard, or enrol in the beginners' batch, before using the deep end. A medical fitness certificate issued within the last 6 months is compulsory for pool registration, and the pool registration fee is Rs. 800 per season.

Separate timings are kept for women students from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM on weekdays. Swimming without the lifeguard on duty is strictly prohibited and leads to suspension of pool access for the rest of the season.

## 5. University teams and sports quota benefits

Selection trials for university teams are held in the second week of August every year. A student selected for a university team receives a sports kit allowance of Rs. 3,000 per year and may apply for duty leave for inter-university tournaments under the Attendance Regulations.

Students who win a medal at an inter-university tournament are awarded 2 activity credits in that semester. Team members must attend at least 80% of the practice sessions; a member who falls below this is dropped from the team by the Sports Officer.
