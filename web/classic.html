How an answer is made

  1. Search the documents

    Your question is matched against every passage twice: by its words and by its meaning. The two rankings are merged.

  2. Check the evidence

    If the best passages don't really cover the question, the help desk stops here and says so.

  3. Answer from the passages

    The answer is written only from the four best passages. Nothing comes from guesswork.

  4. Show the source

    Each claim carries a number. Select it to read the passage, with the supporting sentence marked.
