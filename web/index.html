Methodology

How Uni Help Verifies Campus Truth

A deterministic retrieval architecture designed specifically for high-stakes academic advisement.

1 upload_file

Official Ingestion

Documents (Markdown, text, PDF and Word) are read, split into passages at their headings and indexed.

2 hub

Vector Semantic Search

The question is matched to passages by keywords and by meaning, and the two rankings are merged.

3 task_alt

Cited Answer

Answers are written only from the retrieved passages, with citations that open the source passage. If the passages do not answer the question, Uni Help declines.
